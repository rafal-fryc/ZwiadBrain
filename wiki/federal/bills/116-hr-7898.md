## Summary

PL 116-321 amends the HITECH Act to require HHS to consider whether a HIPAA-covered entity or business associate has adopted recognized cybersecurity practices during the preceding 12 months when determining fines, audit results, and remedies for HIPAA violations — creating a formal incentive to reduce penalties for entities maintaining strong security postures. The law also authorizes HHS's Office of the Inspector General to obtain information and assistance from federal agencies when investigating information-blocking by developers or offerors of health information technology, where information-blocking means practices that interfere with, prevent, or materially discourage access to or exchange of electronic health information.
