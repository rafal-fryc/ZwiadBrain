## Summary

Division Y of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2022 — the Cyber Incident Reporting for Critical Infrastructure Act (CIRCIA) — requires covered private critical-infrastructure entities to report cyber incidents to CISA within 72 hours and ransomware payments within 24 hours of payment. Covered entities must preserve data relevant to reported incidents, and CISA holds subpoena authority to compel reporting. Implementing regulations are subject to notice-and-comment rulemaking, with the final rule pending at the time of enactment.
