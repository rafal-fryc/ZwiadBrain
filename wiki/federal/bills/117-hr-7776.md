## Summary

Enacted as part of the FY2023 National Defense Authorization Act (PL 117-263), the Daniel Anderl Judicial Security and Privacy Act prohibits data brokers from selling, purchasing, or otherwise trading in covered information — including home addresses and license plate numbers — belonging to federal judges and their immediate family members. The law also imposes limitations on the disclosure of such information by government agencies and other parties. Together these provisions restrict the commercial and governmental flow of identifying personal data for the protected class of federal judiciary and their families.
