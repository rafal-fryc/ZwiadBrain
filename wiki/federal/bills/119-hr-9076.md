## Summary

The Postal Data Privacy Act of 2026 was introduced by Rep. Mary Gay Scanlon (D-PA-5) on May 29, 2026, and referred the same day to the House Committee on the Judiciary. The bill amends Chapter 83 of title 18, U.S. Code, to add a new section 1738 barring a governmental entity from using a mail cover (the recording of information appearing on the outside of mail, as defined in 39 C.F.R. § 233.3) unless it first obtains a court order. A court of competent jurisdiction may issue such an order only if the entity offers specific and articulable facts showing reasonable grounds to believe the mail cover is relevant and material to an ongoing criminal investigation, and, for a state authority, only where not prohibited by state law. The bill also requires the Chief Postal Inspector, on request, to preserve relevant records for 90 days (extendable for an additional 90 days) pending a court order. The bill remains in early committee stage with no further action taken.
