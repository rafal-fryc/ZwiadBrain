## Summary

Using the S. 1318 vehicle (originally a bill on military grave markers), the House passed substitute text combining two major provisions: it reauthorizes FISA Section 702 surveillance authority through April 2029 while tightening oversight of FBI queries involving U.S. persons, including monthly ODNI reviews, mandatory IC Inspector General referrals for violations, criminal penalties for falsifying compliance, and a restriction limiting query approvals to certain FBI attorneys. The same bill prohibits Federal Reserve banks from offering accounts or financial products directly to individuals and bars the Federal Reserve from issuing, testing, or developing a central bank digital currency (CBDC). The Senate rejected a motion to proceed to the House-passed version 47–52 on June 5, 2026, leaving the bill stalled.
