## Summary

The TAKE IT DOWN Act (Public Law 119-12, signed May 19, 2025) criminalizes the nonconsensual online publication of intimate images of real people, including AI-generated deepfakes, for both adults and minors. Covered platforms — public websites and apps hosting user-generated content — must establish a takedown process and remove flagged content within 48 hours of a valid request; threats to publish such material are also prohibited. The FTC enforces the platform removal obligations, which took effect May 19, 2026. This law is significant for AI and privacy tracking because it is one of the first federal statutes to explicitly treat synthetic, computer-generated intimate imagery the same as authentic imagery under criminal and platform-liability law.
