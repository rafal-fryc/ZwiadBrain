## Summary

The FTC charged mResource LLC (doing business as Loop Works LLC), a recruitment and talent management company, with deceiving consumers by continuing to claim active EU-U.S. Privacy Shield participation on its website after its certification lapsed in December 2017. The matter was resolved through a consent order issued on November 15, 2018. The order prohibits mResource from misrepresenting its participation in any government or self-regulatory privacy or security program and carries a 20-year term.

## Background

mResource self-certified to the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield framework in December 2016. When its certification expired in December 2017, the company failed to complete renewal steps but continued displaying statements on its website claiming current Privacy Shield participation and adherence to Privacy Shield Principles.

## Allegations

The FTC alleged one count of deceptive trade practices under Section 5(a) of the FTC Act: mResource falsely represented, expressly or by implication, that it was a current participant in the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield framework after its certification had lapsed without renewal.

## Outcome

A consent order (Docket C-4663) was issued on November 15, 2018, prohibiting mResource from misrepresenting membership in or adherence to any government or self-regulatory privacy or security program, including the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Privacy Shield frameworks. The order imposes compliance reporting, recordkeeping, and monitoring obligations and terminates November 15, 2038.

## Significance

This action is part of the FTC's ongoing enforcement sweep against companies that falsely claimed active Privacy Shield certification after allowing their registrations to lapse, reinforcing that expired certification must be promptly removed from public-facing materials and that continued claims constitute deceptive practices actionable under Section 5.
