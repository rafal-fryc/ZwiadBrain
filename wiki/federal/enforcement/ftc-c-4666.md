## Summary

The FTC charged SmartStart Employment Screening, Inc. with falsely claiming active participation in the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield framework for nearly a year after its certification lapsed in September 2017. The company also failed to affirm to the Department of Commerce that it would continue protecting personal data collected during its participation after withdrawal. The matter was resolved through a 20-year consent order prohibiting Privacy Shield misrepresentations and requiring SmartStart to either affirm continuing data protections or delete the affected personal information.

## Developments

- 2018-11-15 — Consent order issued prohibiting SmartStart from misrepresenting Privacy Shield participation and requiring it to affirm continuing data protections or delete EU personal data collected under the lapsed certification. — [source](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/182-3154-smartstart-employment-screening-inc-matter)

## Background

SmartStart obtained EU-U.S. Privacy Shield certification in September 2016 but failed to renew when it expired in September 2017. Despite the lapsed certification, the company continued to display claims of current Privacy Shield participation on its website through at least June 2018, and never affirmed to the Department of Commerce that it would continue applying Privacy Shield principles to personal data collected during its participation.

## Allegations

The FTC alleged two counts: (1) that SmartStart misrepresented its current participation in the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield framework after its certification had lapsed; and (2) that SmartStart misrepresented that it would abide by Privacy Shield obligations, including the requirement to affirm continuing data protections to the Department of Commerce upon withdrawal from the program.

## Outcome

SmartStart agreed to a 20-year consent order (Docket C-4666) prohibiting misrepresentations about participation in any government- or self-regulatory-sponsored privacy or security program. The order required SmartStart, within 10 days of the effective date, to either affirm continuing application of Privacy Shield principles to previously collected EU personal data or return or delete that data. The order also imposed compliance reporting, recordkeeping, and monitoring obligations for 20 years, terminating November 15, 2038.

## Significance

This action is one of a series of FTC enforcement matters targeting companies that falsely claimed Privacy Shield membership after their certifications lapsed, reinforcing that Privacy Shield participation claims are materially deceptive once certification expires and that the obligation to protect data collected under the framework continues even after withdrawal.
