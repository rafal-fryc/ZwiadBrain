## Summary

The FTC charged DCR Workforce, Inc. with falsely claiming active participation in the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield framework after its certification had lapsed in February 2018. The Florida-based workforce management software provider continued displaying Privacy Shield compliance representations on its website despite the expired certification. The matter was resolved through a 20-year consent order prohibiting future misrepresentations about participation in privacy programs.

## Background

DCR Workforce, Inc. is a Florida-based provider of workforce management software. The company obtained Privacy Shield certification in January 2017, but that certification lapsed by February 2018. Despite the lapse, DCR Workforce continued to represent on its website that it was a current participant in the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield framework and had certified to the Department of Commerce that it adheres to the Privacy Shield Principles.

## Allegations

The FTC alleged one count of Privacy Misrepresentation under Section 5(a) of the FTC Act, charging that DCR Workforce falsely represented — directly or by implication — that it was a current participant in the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield framework when its certification had lapsed, constituting a deceptive act or practice.

## Outcome

DCR Workforce was placed under a 20-year consent order (Docket C-4698) prohibiting it from misrepresenting participation in or compliance with any privacy or security program, including the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Privacy Shield frameworks. The order also requires compliance reporting, recordkeeping for 20 years, and ongoing FTC monitoring. The order terminates January 13, 2040.

## Significance

This action is part of the FTC's sustained enforcement campaign against companies that falsely claim Privacy Shield certification. It reinforces that allowing a certification to lapse while maintaining compliance representations on a website constitutes an actionable deceptive practice under Section 5, even absent any underlying data misuse.
