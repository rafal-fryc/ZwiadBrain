## Summary

The FTC charged LotaData, Inc., a mobile location data analytics company, with falsely claiming on its website that it had certified to the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Privacy Shield Frameworks. Although LotaData initiated a certification application in November 2017, it never completed the required steps to participate in either framework. The matter resolved via a 20-year consent order prohibiting future misrepresentations about participation in privacy or security programs.

## Developments

## Background

LotaData initiated a Privacy Shield certification application with the U.S. Department of Commerce in November 2017 but never completed the necessary steps to participate in either the EU-U.S. or Swiss-U.S. Privacy Shield Framework. Despite this, the company continued to publish statements on its website privacy policy asserting it had certified to the Privacy Shield Principles.

## Allegations

The FTC alleged a single count of Privacy Shield Misrepresentation under Section 5(a) of the FTC Act, charging that LotaData falsely represented, directly or by implication, that it was a participant in the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Privacy Shield frameworks when it had never completed certification.

## Outcome

LotaData agreed to a consent order (Docket C-4700) effective January 15, 2020, terminating January 13, 2040. The order prohibits misrepresentations about membership in, certification by, or participation in any government or self-regulatory privacy or security program, including Privacy Shield. It also imposes acknowledgment, compliance reporting, recordkeeping for 10 years, and ongoing compliance monitoring obligations.

## Significance

This action is one of a series of FTC enforcement matters targeting companies that falsely claim Privacy Shield certification — a deceptive practice that misleads EU and Swiss data subjects about the data-protection safeguards applied to their personal information. It reinforces that initiating but not completing a certification process provides no basis for claiming certified status.
