## Summary

The FTC charged T&M Protection Resources, LLC, a background check and security services company, with falsely claiming active participation in the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield framework after its certification lapsed in 2018. The company also failed to obtain required annual verification statements while certified and did not affirm continuing data-protection obligations after leaving the program. The matter was resolved through a 20-year consent order prohibiting future misrepresentations about privacy program participation.

## Developments

- 2020-03-15 — Final consent order issued against T&M Protection Resources for falsely claiming active EU-U.S. Privacy Shield participation after its certification lapsed and failing to meet verification and continuing-obligation requirements. — [source](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/192-3092-tm-protection-resources-llc-matter)

## Background

T&M Protection Resources obtained EU-U.S. Privacy Shield certification in 2017 but failed to renew it upon expiration in 2018. Despite Commerce warnings, the company continued displaying Privacy Shield claims on its website. It also failed to obtain required annual verification statements attesting that its privacy practices were true and implemented, and after certification lapsed it did not affirm to Commerce that it would continue protecting personal information collected during participation.

## Allegations

The FTC alleged three counts under FTC Act Section 5: (1) falsely representing active participation in the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield framework after certification had lapsed; (2) misrepresenting compliance with EU-U.S. Privacy Shield principles; and (3) misrepresenting compliance with the continuing obligations under the framework, specifically the requirement to affirm to Commerce that personal data received during participation would continue to be protected.

## Outcome

A final consent order (Docket C-4709) was issued, effective March 15, 2020, with a 20-year duration terminating March 16, 2040. The order prohibits misrepresentations about participation in or compliance with any government- or self-regulatory-sponsored privacy or security program. It requires T&M to either affirm continuing Privacy Shield data-protection obligations or return/delete the affected personal information within ten days. The order also imposes compliance reporting, recordkeeping for ten years, and FTC monitoring authority.

## Significance

This action is part of the FTC's ongoing enforcement of Privacy Shield certification misrepresentation cases, reinforcing that companies must remove privacy framework claims promptly upon losing certification and must fulfill post-participation data-protection obligations even after leaving a program.
