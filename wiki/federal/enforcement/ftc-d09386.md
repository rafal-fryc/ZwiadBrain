## Summary

The FTC charged Raging Wire Data Centers, Inc. with misrepresenting its participation in or compliance with international privacy frameworks, including the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield and Swiss-U.S. Privacy Shield. The matter was resolved through a consent order requiring the company to cease such misrepresentations, obtain annual third-party compliance reviews while self-certified under Privacy Shield, and fulfill continuing data-protection obligations upon any lapse in certification. The order carries a 20-year duration, terminating October 28, 2040.

## Allegations

The FTC alleged that Raging Wire Data Centers misrepresented its membership in, adherence to, compliance with, or participation in government- or self-regulatory-sponsored privacy or security programs, including the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield, Swiss-U.S. Privacy Shield, and APEC Cross-Border Privacy Rules, in violation of FTC Act Section 5.

## Outcome

Raging Wire Data Centers entered a consent order prohibiting future misrepresentations about privacy program participation or compliance. The order requires annual independent third-party assessments while the company holds Privacy Shield self-certification, mandates continuing Privacy Shield data-protection obligations upon any withdrawal or lapse in certification, and imposes recordkeeping, compliance reporting, and monitoring obligations for 20 years.

## Significance

The action is part of the FTC's sustained enforcement campaign against companies that falsely claim participation in or compliance with the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield and related cross-border data transfer frameworks. It reinforces that Privacy Shield certification misrepresentations are actionable deception under FTC Act Section 5 and establishes a template requiring ongoing independent audits as a structural remedy.
