## Summary

Arkansas HB 1876 (Act 927 of 2025) adds Ark. Code § 18-4-101, assigning ownership of generative-AI output and trained models. A person who supplies the input or directive to a generative AI tool owns the generated content, provided it does not infringe existing copyrights or intellectual property rights; a person who supplies the data or input to train a model owns the resulting trained model, provided the training data was lawfully acquired and ownership has not been transferred by contract. Where an employee is directed to use a generative AI tool for model training or content generation as part of their duties, the resulting training data and generated content are the employer's property under a work-made-for-hire rule.
