## Summary

AB 1130 amends California's data-breach notification law (Civil Code sections 1798.29, 1798.81.5, and 1798.82), updating the requirements businesses and agencies must follow when notifying consumers of breaches involving personal information. It was enacted as Chapter 750, Statutes of 2019.
