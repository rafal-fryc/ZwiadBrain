## Summary

AB 1355 makes broad amendments to the CCPA, modifying multiple Civil Code sections covering consumer rights to access, deletion, and opt-out, business obligations, and enforcement provisions. The bill addresses treatment of personal information across the Act's core operative sections and was enacted as Chapter 757, Statutes of 2019.
