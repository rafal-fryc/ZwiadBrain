## Summary

California AB 2273 (Chapter 320, Statutes of 2022) enacts the California Age-Appropriate Design Code Act, adding Title 1.81.47 to the Civil Code and imposing consumer-privacy obligations on businesses that provide online services or products likely to be accessed by children, including requirements around default privacy settings and data protection assessments for minors.
