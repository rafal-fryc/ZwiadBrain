## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

AB 335, authored by Assemblymember Tasha Boerner Horvath with bipartisan coauthors Patrick O'Donnell, Freddie Rodriguez, Patricia Bates, and Brian Jones, was introduced on January 27, 2021, and referred to the Assembly Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection in February 2021. The bill mirrors the existing CCPA vehicle-information exemption under Civil Code §1798.145, extending it to vessel dealers and manufacturers sharing hull identification, model, and ownership information for federally mandated warranty or recall purposes. The Assembly Privacy and Consumer Protection Committee passed it unanimously 11-0 on April 8, 2021, and the Assembly Appropriations Committee placed it on the consent calendar (14-0 on April 21, 2021); the full Assembly passed it 74-1 on May 3, 2021. In the Senate, the Judiciary Committee unanimously approved it 11-0 on June 8, 2021, re-referring it to Appropriations; after Senate amendments, the Senate passed it 38-0 on September 8, 2021, and the Assembly concurred 77-0 on September 9, 2021. The bill was enrolled, presented to the Governor on September 17, 2021, and chaptered by the Secretary of State on October 8, 2021, as Chapter 700, Statutes of 2021.
