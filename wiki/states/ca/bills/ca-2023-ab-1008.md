## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

AB 1008, authored by Assemblymember Rebecca Bauer-Kahan (D-Orinda), was introduced on February 15, 2023, originally as a conservation measure; it was substantially amended in mid-2024 to address the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 (CCPA), clarifying that 'personal information' can exist in physical, digital, and abstract formats — including artificial-intelligence systems capable of outputting personal information — and that biometric data collected without a consumer's knowledge does not qualify as 'publicly available.' After passing the Assembly floor 61-15 on May 31, 2023 under its original subject, the bill was held in the Senate during 2023 and then transformed through committee amendments in 2024. The Senate Judiciary Committee voted 9-2 on July 2, 2024 to pass the amended bill; the California Privacy Protection Agency voted at its July 16, 2024 board meeting to take a formal support position. The Senate passed the bill 29-9 on August 30, 2024, and the Assembly concurred in Senate amendments 55-15 on August 31, 2024. Governor Gavin Newsom signed AB 1008 on September 28, 2024, enacting it as Chapter 802, Statutes of 2024, effective January 1, 2025 — among a broader 2024 package of generative-AI and privacy laws.
