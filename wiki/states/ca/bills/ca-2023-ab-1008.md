## Summary

California AB 1008 (Chapter 802, Statutes of 2024) amends the CCPA's definitions section (Civil Code 1798.140) to clarify the scope of what constitutes personal information under California law. The amendment addresses how the definition applies to information held or processed by businesses covered by the CCPA.
