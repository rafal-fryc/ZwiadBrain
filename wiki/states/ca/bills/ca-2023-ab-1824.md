## Summary

California AB 1824 (Chapter 940, Statutes of 2024) amends Civil Code Section 1798.120 to strengthen the CCPA consumer opt-out right in the context of business mergers and acquisitions. It requires that a consumer's opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information be honored by a successor business following such a transaction.
