## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

AB 1824, introduced on January 11, 2024 by Assemblymember Avelino Valencia, amended Civil Code §1798.120 to require any business receiving consumer personal information through a merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, or similar transaction to honor opt-out-of-sale-and-sharing directions the consumer had previously given the original business. The Assembly Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection passed it unanimously 11-0 on April 2, 2024, finding it a reasonable clarification of an ambiguity in the CCPA consistent with the CPRA's consumer-protective purpose; the Assembly Appropriations Committee cleared it 14-0 on April 24, 2024, and the full Assembly passed it on consent 73-0 on May 2, 2024. In the Senate, the Judiciary Committee — which noted the bill was motivated in part by privacy concerns arising from Facebook's 2014 acquisition of WhatsApp — passed it 11-0 on June 4, 2024, and after Senate Appropriations cleared it, the Senate passed it 40-0 on August 27, 2024. The Assembly concurred in Senate amendments 77-0 on August 28, 2024, and Governor Gavin Newsom signed the measure on September 29, 2024, enacting it as Chapter 940, Statutes of 2024, effective January 1, 2025.
