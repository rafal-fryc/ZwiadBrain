## Summary

AB 1836 amends California Civil Code §3344.1 to prohibit the commercial use of AI-generated digital replicas of deceased personalities in audiovisual works or sound recordings without consent from the deceased's estate. Liability attaches to producers and distributors who use such replicas without authorization. The law extends existing personality rights protections to cover AI-generated likenesses of the dead.
