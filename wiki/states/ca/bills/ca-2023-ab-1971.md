## Summary

California AB 1971 (Chapter 508, Statutes of 2024) adds a new chapter to the Business and Professions Code (commencing with Section 22585.5) establishing personal-information protections specific to the administration of standardized tests. The law regulates how entities involved in standardized-test administration may collect, use, and disclose test-takers' personal information.
