## Summary

AB 2602 renders unenforceable any contract provision permitting use of a digital replica of a performer's voice or likeness to substitute for work the performer would otherwise have performed, unless the provision includes a reasonably specific description of the intended uses. The enforceability exception also requires that the performer was represented by an attorney or a union at the time of contracting. The law targets agreements that effectively authorize open-ended AI replacement of live performer work.
