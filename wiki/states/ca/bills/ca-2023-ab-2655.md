## Summary

California AB 2655 — the Defending Democracy from Deepfake Deception Act of 2024 (Chapter 261, Statutes of 2024) — adds Elections Code Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 20510) to require large online platforms to remove or label materially deceptive AI-generated content depicting candidates or election officials in election-related contexts. It also amends the Code of Civil Procedure to provide an enforcement mechanism for affected parties.
