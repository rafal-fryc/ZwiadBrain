## Summary

California AB 3030 (Chapter 848, Statutes of 2024) adds Health and Safety Code Chapter 2.13 (commencing with Section 1339.75) requiring health care providers and facilities to disclose to patients when generative AI has been used to generate certain patient-facing communications. The law ensures patients know when AI, rather than a licensed clinician, produced health-related messages they receive.

## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

AB 3030 was introduced by Assemblymember Lisa Calderon on February 16, 2024, to require health facilities, clinics, and physician offices that deploy generative AI to generate patient communications concerning clinical information to include a disclaimer that the communication was AI-generated and clear instructions for reaching a human health care provider. The bill cleared the Assembly Committee on Health unanimously (16–0) on April 9, 2024, then the Assembly Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection 10–0 on April 23, 2024, before the Assembly Appropriations Committee approved it 15–0 on May 8, 2024, and the full Assembly passed it 71–0 on May 20, 2024. In the Senate, the Health Committee voted 11–0 on June 26, 2024, and the Appropriations Committee approved it 7–0 on August 15, 2024; Senate committee amendments narrowed the disclosure requirement to AI-generated communications not reviewed by a licensed or certified health care provider, resolving opposition from the California Hospital Association, the California Medical Association, the California Dental Association, and the California Radiology Society. The Senate passed the amended measure 40–0 on August 30, 2024, and the Assembly concurred 75–0 on August 31, 2024, with Assemblymember Cecilia Aguiar-Curry presenting on Calderon's behalf. Governor Gavin Newsom signed AB 3030 on September 28, 2024, enacting it as Chapter 848, Statutes of 2024, effective January 1, 2025.
