## Summary

California AB 3286 (Chapter 121, Statutes of 2024) amends more than a dozen Civil Code CCPA provisions to update the monetary thresholds used in the statute — such as those triggering business coverage and civil penalty ranges — and makes related adjustments to grant provisions administered under the law. The changes reflect inflation-based recalibration of the dollar figures embedded throughout the CCPA.
