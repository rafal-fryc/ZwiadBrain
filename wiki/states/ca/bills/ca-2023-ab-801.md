## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

AB 801 (Patterson) was introduced on February 13, 2023, as a two-year bill to close a gap in California's student privacy framework by requiring SOPIPA operators to delete a pupil's covered information — information falling outside the California Consumer Privacy Act — upon request of a parent, guardian, or eligible former pupil who has disenrolled from an LEA. The bill passed the Assembly Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection unanimously (11–0) on March 21, 2023, and, after a one-year hold while the author worked with committee staff on amendments addressing mandatory pupil records and the qualifying age for independent requests (raised from 16 to 18), cleared the Assembly Committee on Education (7–0) on January 10, 2024. The full Assembly passed it 67–0 on January 18, 2024. In the Senate, AB 801 advanced on consent through the Senate Education Committee (7–0) on June 5, 2024, then received a substantive hearing in the Senate Judiciary Committee (11–0) on June 11, 2024, with no registered opposition at either committee. The Senate passed the bill 39–0 on August 28, 2024, and the Assembly concurred in Senate amendments 76–0 on August 29, 2024. Governor Newsom signed the measure on September 29, 2024, enacting it as Chapter 935, Statutes of 2024.
