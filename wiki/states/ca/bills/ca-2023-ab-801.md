## Summary

California AB 801 (Chapter 935, Statutes of 2024) amends the Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (SOPIPA) by updating Sections 22584 and 22586 of the Business and Professions Code. The law governs how operators of online services directed at K-12 students may collect, use, and disclose student personal information, and this amendment adjusts those protections and the scope of covered operators or information.
