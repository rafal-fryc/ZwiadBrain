## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

AB 947 was introduced on February 14, 2023, by Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel, who also served as Chair of the Assembly Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection, with coauthors Blanca Rubio, Stephanie Nguyen, and Anna Caballero. The bill made a single, targeted amendment to Civil Code § 1798.140, adding a consumer's citizenship or immigration status to the California Consumer Privacy Act's definition of "sensitive personal information" — closing a gap that the Electronic Frontier Foundation had flagged as an oversight when voters enacted the California Privacy Rights Act through Proposition 24 in 2020. The Assembly Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection passed the bill on consent on April 25, 2023 (Ayes 11, Noes 0), followed by the Assembly Appropriations Committee (Ayes 14, Noes 0) and the full Assembly floor on May 22, 2023 (Ayes 76, Noes 0). In the Senate, the bill cleared the Senate Judiciary Committee on June 20, 2023 (Ayes 11, Noes 0) with unanimous bipartisan support, passed through Senate Appropriations, and was read for the third time on the Senate floor on September 5, 2023 (Ayes 39, Noes 0). Governor Gavin Newsom signed and chaptered the bill on October 8, 2023, as Chapter 551, Statutes of 2023, effective January 1, 2024. The bill encountered no registered opposition at any stage and was supported by Oakland Privacy, the Electronic Frontier Foundation, and the California Privacy Protection Agency.
