## Summary

California AB 947 (Chapter 551, Statutes of 2023) amends Civil Code Section 1798.140 — the CCPA's definitions section — to expand the categories or handling rules for sensitive personal information. The change affects what businesses must treat as sensitive under the CCPA and the corresponding consumer rights that attach to that classification.
