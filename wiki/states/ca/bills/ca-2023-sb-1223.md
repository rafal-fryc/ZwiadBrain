## Summary

California SB 1223 (Chapter 887, Statutes of 2024) amends the CCPA's definitions section (Civil Code 1798.140) to add neural data — information generated by measuring a person's brain or neural activity — to the statutory categories of sensitive personal information. Businesses that collect neural data from California consumers are thereby subject to the CCPA's heightened sensitive-information obligations.
