    coauthors Senators Scott Wiener and Dave Min and Assemblymembers Jesse Gabriel,
    Gregg Hart, Josh Lowenthal, and Buffy Wicks; the bill, branded the DELETE Act,
    proposed to transfer California's data-broker registry from the Attorney General
    to the California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA) and to require the CPPA to
    build a single, free, accessible deletion mechanism through which a consumer could
    direct every registered data broker to delete their personal information in one
    verifiable request. The Senate Judiciary Committee (Chair Thomas Umberg) heard
    the bill on April 25, 2023, and voted 9-2 to pass it; after Senate Appropriations
    clearance, the full Senate passed SB 362 on May 31, 2023, by 32-8. In the Assembly,
    the Assembly Privacy and Consumer Protection Committee voted 7-0 on June 27, 2023,
    and the Assembly Appropriations Committee cleared the bill 10-3 on September 1,
    2023 following amendments that extended the deletion-processing window from 30
    to 45 days and set the portal's two deadlines — the California Privacy Protection
    Agency must establish the deletion portal by January 1, 2026, and registered data

    The Assembly passed

    Gavin Newsom signed SB 362 into law on October 10, 2023, as Chapter 709, Statutes

## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

Senator Josh Becker introduced SB 362 on February 8, 2023, with principal coauthors Senators Scott Wiener and Dave Min and Assemblymembers Jesse Gabriel, Gregg Hart, Josh Lowenthal, and Buffy Wicks; the bill, branded the DELETE Act, proposed to transfer California's data-broker registry from the Attorney General to the California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA) and to require the CPPA to build a single, free, accessible deletion mechanism through which a consumer could direct every registered data broker to delete their personal information in one verifiable request. The Senate Judiciary Committee (Chair Thomas Umberg) heard the bill on April 25, 2023, and voted 9-2 to pass it; after Senate Appropriations clearance, the full Senate passed SB 362 on May 31, 2023, by 32-8. In the Assembly, the Assembly Privacy and Consumer Protection Committee voted 7-0 on June 27, 2023, and the Assembly Appropriations Committee cleared the bill 10-3 on September 1, 2023 following amendments that extended the deletion-processing window from 30 to 45 days and set the portal's two deadlines — the California Privacy Protection Agency must establish the deletion portal by January 1, 2026, and registered data brokers must begin accessing it to process deletion requests by August 1, 2026. The Assembly passed the amended bill 52-14 on September 13, 2023—the final day of the regular session—and the Senate concurred in the Assembly amendments 31-9 on September 14, 2023. Governor Gavin Newsom signed SB 362 into law on October 10, 2023, as Chapter 709, Statutes of 2023, making California the first jurisdiction globally to mandate a single, centralized consumer deletion portal covering all registered data brokers.
