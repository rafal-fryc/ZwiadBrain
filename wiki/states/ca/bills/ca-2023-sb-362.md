## Summary

California SB 362 — the DELETE Act (Chapter 709, Statutes of 2023) — strengthens the state's data-broker registration framework by amending existing Civil Code provisions and adding new sections (1798.99.85-.89) to direct creation of a single, accessible online mechanism through which consumers can submit one deletion request that applies to all registered data brokers. Data brokers are required to honor deletion requests received through this centralized mechanism.
