## Summary

California SB 793 (Chapter 184, Statutes of 2023) adds Insurance Code Section 791.045 to impose privacy-notice requirements on insurers regarding the personal information they collect, use, and disclose. The law establishes obligations for how insurance entities must inform consumers about their personal-information practices under California law.
