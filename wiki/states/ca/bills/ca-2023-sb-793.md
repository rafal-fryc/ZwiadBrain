## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

SB 793 was introduced by Senator Steve Glazer on February 17, 2023, with Assemblymember Avelino Valencia as principal coauthor in the Assembly. The bill proposed to add Section 791.045 to the Insurance Code, aligning California's Insurance Information and Privacy Protection Act with the 2015 FAST Act amendment to the federal Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act — allowing insurers to forgo sending a full privacy notice annually when their underlying policy had not changed, provided they made the policy available online and delivered an abbreviated annual reminder of consumer rights. After Senate amendments on April 10 and April 17, 2023, the Senate Standing Committee on Insurance heard the bill on April 12, 2023, and passed it 7–0, referring it to the Committee on Judiciary; the Judiciary Committee approved it on April 21, 2023, and the Senate Rules Committee filed its floor analysis on May 10, 2023. The bill then passed the Senate and was transmitted to the Assembly, where it cleared the Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection on the consent calendar (hearing June 13, 2023), the Committee on Insurance (hearing June 28, 2023), and the Committee on Appropriations (August 14, 2023), before passing the full Assembly on a 73–0 vote on August 24, 2023. Enrolled and presented to the Governor on August 30, 2023, SB 793 was signed by Governor Gavin Newsom on September 8, 2023, and chaptered as Chapter 184, Statutes of 2023.
