## Summary

The California AI Transparency Act requires providers of generative AI systems with more than one million monthly users to offer a free AI-detection tool and to embed machine-readable disclosures identifying AI-generated content. Covered providers must also give users the option of a visible, manifest disclosure on AI-generated outputs. Civil penalties of $5,000 per day per violation apply, with the law originally set to take effect January 1, 2026.
