## Summary

This bill would have prohibited an operator of a companion chatbot from making the chatbot available to a child, together with related safety duties; the operator's knowledge standard for a user's age was set to phase in on January 1, 2027. The obligations fell on operators of these companion AI products, not on developers — the "directed at or likely to be used by children" framing reflected an earlier version of the bill and did not survive into the enrolled (vetoed) text. The bill would have added these requirements to California's Business and Professions Code as a new chapter governing AI products and minors. It was vetoed by the Governor, and the legislature subsequently dropped further consideration of an override.
