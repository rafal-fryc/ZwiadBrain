## Summary

California's Information Practices Act of 1977 governs how state agencies collect, maintain, use, and disclose personal information. This bill amends 15 sections of that statute across Civil Code Sections 1798.3 through 1798.68, updating definitions and revising requirements around data handling, notice to individuals, and disclosure of personal information held by agencies. The bill has passed one chamber and is currently in committee in the second, re-referred to the Committee on Privacy, Data, Technology, and Consumer Protection.
