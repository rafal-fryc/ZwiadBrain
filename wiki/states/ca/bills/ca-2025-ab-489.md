## Summary

AB 489 makes it unlawful for an AI or generative-AI system's advertising or functionality to use titles, letters, or phrases (such as 'doctor' or 'nurse') that falsely indicate or imply the care, advice, or assessments it provides come from a licensed human health care professional. Enforcement runs through the same licensing boards that police unlicensed practice under the Business and Professions Code, with each prohibited use of a protected term counted as a separate violation. It applies to entities that develop or deploy such AI/GenAI tools and was chaptered October 11, 2025.
