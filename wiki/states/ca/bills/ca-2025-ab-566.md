## Summary

AB 566, the California Opt Me Out Act, adds Section 1798.136 to the Civil Code to require, beginning January 1, 2027, that any business developing or maintaining a web browser include an easily configurable feature letting consumers send an opt-out preference signal to businesses they interact with online, refusing sale/sharing of their personal information under the CCPA. Browser makers must clearly disclose how the signal works and are immunized from liability if a business that receives the signal fails to honor it; the California Privacy Protection Agency may adopt implementing regulations. Chaptered October 8, 2025.
