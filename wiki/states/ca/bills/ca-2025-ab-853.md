## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

Assemblymember Buffy Wicks introduced AB 853, the California AI Transparency Act amendments, on February 19, 2025, with coauthors Assemblymember Rick Zbur and Senator Josh Becker, to expand the existing CAITA framework to large online platforms, GenAI system hosting platforms, and capture-device manufacturers. In the Assembly, the bill cleared the Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection (Ayes 11, Noes 1, 4/23/25) and the Committee on Judiciary (Ayes 9, Noes 0, 4/30/25) before the Committee on Appropriations amended and passed it (Ayes 11, Noes 0, 5/23/25); the Assembly floor then passed it on third reading 58-2 on June 2, 2025, sending it to the Senate. There, the Senate Committee on Judiciary amended and passed the bill (Ayes 11, Noes 0, 7/16/25) and the Senate Committee on Appropriations reported it out of the suspense file (Ayes 5, Noes 2, 8/29/25) before the full Senate passed it on third reading 30-6 on September 11, 2025. The Assembly then concurred in the Senate amendments 60-4 on September 12, 2025, and the bill was enrolled and presented to Governor Gavin Newsom on September 24, 2025. Governor Newsom signed AB 853 on October 13, 2025, enacting it as Chapter 674, Statutes of 2025, which pushes CAITA's operative date to August 2, 2026 and phases in provenance-disclosure duties for large platforms and GenAI hosting platforms (January 1, 2027) and capture-device manufacturers (January 1, 2028).
