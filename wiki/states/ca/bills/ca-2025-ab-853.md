## Summary

AB 853 amends the California AI Transparency Act, pushing its operative date to August 2, 2026, and layering on new provenance-disclosure duties: beginning January 1, 2027, large online platforms (over 2 million monthly users) must detect and let users inspect standards-compliant AI provenance data and must not strip it from content, and GenAI hosting platforms may not knowingly make available a GenAI system lacking required disclosures. Beginning January 1, 2028, capture-device manufacturers must offer (and by default embed) a latent disclosure identifying the device and creation date/time in captured content. Violations are enforceable by the Attorney General or local prosecutors for $5,000 per violation per day, and the act is chaptered October 13, 2025.
