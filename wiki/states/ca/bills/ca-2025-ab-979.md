## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

AB 979, authored by Assemblymember Jacqui Irwin with Senator Jerry McNerney as coauthor, was introduced on February 20, 2025 to direct the California Cybersecurity Integration Center (Cal-CSIC) to develop, by January 1, 2027, a California AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook — modeled on the federal Joint Cyber Defense Collaborative's AI playbook — to facilitate information sharing on AI-related cyber threats and to require state AI contractors and vendors to report known vulnerabilities, with confidentiality protections for shared threat data. The bill cleared its first policy committee, the Assembly Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection, on a 13-0 vote on May 1, 2025, and Assembly Appropriations on a 14-0 vote on May 23, 2025, before passing the full Assembly on third reading by a unanimous 78-0 vote on June 2, 2025. In the Senate, the bill was approved by the Committee on Governmental Organization (15-0, June 24, 2025) and the Committee on Judiciary (13-0, July 1, 2025), then held on the Senate Appropriations suspense file (7-0, August 18, 2025) before being sent to the floor with a do-pass recommendation (7-0, August 29, 2025). The Senate passed AB 979 on special consent by a 40-0 vote on September 11, 2025, and the Assembly concurred in the Senate's amendments by a 79-0 vote on September 12, 2025, reflecting bipartisan, largely uncontested support throughout the process. The bill was enrolled and presented to Governor Gavin Newsom on September 24, 2025, and he signed it on October 3, 2025, chaptering it as Chapter 285, Statutes of 2025.
