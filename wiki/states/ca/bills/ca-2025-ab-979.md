## Summary

AB 979 amends the statute governing the California Cybersecurity Integration Center (Cal-CSIC) to formally incorporate artificial intelligence into the center's cybersecurity mission. The Cal-CSIC is directed to account for AI-related threats and capabilities in its threat analysis, coordination, and information-sharing work. The bill was signed by the Governor on October 3, 2025, and is now law as Chapter 285, Statutes of 2025.
