## Summary

SB 278 amends Health and Safety Code Section 120985 to let health care providers disclose identifying HIV test results of Medi-Cal beneficiaries, without the patient's written authorization, to the beneficiary's assigned Medi-Cal managed care plan and to external quality review organizations auditing those plans, solely for administering HIV-related quality-improvement and value-based payment programs. Non-identifying HIV results may similarly flow from a managed care plan to state Department of Health Care Services staff for the same purpose, while the existing general ban on disclosing HIV results to unauthorized third parties remains otherwise intact. Chaptered October 13, 2025.
