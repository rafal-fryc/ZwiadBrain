## Summary

California's SB 354 overhauls the Insurance Information and Privacy Protection Act by rewriting and expanding the rules governing how insurers, agents, and data-support organizations collect, use, share, and safeguard personal information about applicants and policyholders. The bill tightens consent and notice requirements, strengthens individuals' rights to access and correct their insurance data, and adds new limits on when that data may be disclosed to third parties. It passed the Senate 28–10 and is awaiting action in the Assembly Insurance Committee.
