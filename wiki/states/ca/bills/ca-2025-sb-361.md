## Summary

SB 361 expands California's data broker registration law by requiring brokers to disclose to the California Privacy Protection Agency far more detail about the categories of data they collect (names, birthdates, ZIP codes, government ID numbers, biometric, geolocation, reproductive health, sexual orientation, gender identity, union membership, citizenship data, etc.) and whether they sold or shared consumer data in the past year with a foreign adversary, the federal government, other states, law enforcement, or a GenAI developer. It shortens the deadline for processing a denied deletion request (via the accessible deletion mechanism) to 45 days, and bars the Agency from publicly posting certain sensitive collection-category disclosures on its website. Chaptered October 8, 2025.
