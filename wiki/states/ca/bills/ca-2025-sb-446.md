## Summary

SB 446 amends Civil Code Section 1798.82 to impose a firm deadline on California's data breach notification law: businesses must now notify affected California residents within 30 calendar days of discovering a breach (delay still allowed for law enforcement needs or to determine breach scope/restore system integrity), rather than the prior open-ended 'most expedient time possible' standard. It also requires that, when notification of more than 500 residents is legally required, the sample notice submitted to the Attorney General must be filed within 15 calendar days after residents are notified. Chaptered October 3, 2025.
