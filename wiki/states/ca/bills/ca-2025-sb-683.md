## Summary

SB 683 amends Civil Code Section 3344, California's right-of-publicity statute, to let a person whose name, voice, signature, photograph, or likeness is used without consent for advertising or sales seek an injunction or temporary restraining order in addition to existing damages remedies. If a court grants an ex parte TRO ordering removal or cessation of the unauthorized use, the respondent must comply within two business days of being served, absent a different deadline in the order. The bill also contains a contingent amendment that would take effect instead if a companion bill, SB 11, is enacted and takes effect first. Chaptered October 10, 2025.
