## Summary

California Senate Bill 833 requires human oversight of artificial intelligence systems used in connection with the state's critical infrastructure, adding Article 6.6 to the Government Code (starting at §8954.50). The bill establishes human-in-the-loop control requirements, meaning AI systems operating in critical-infrastructure contexts cannot act autonomously without a human in the decision chain. It passed the Senate unanimously 37-0 and is pending in the Assembly Appropriations Committee.
