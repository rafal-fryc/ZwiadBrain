## Summary

Colorado HB19-1197 makes it a class 1 misdemeanor to knowingly publish a child-welfare caseworker's personal information online when doing so poses an imminent and serious safety threat to the caseworker or their family and the person knew or should have known of that threat. It also requires government agencies to remove a caseworker's personal information from publicly available online records upon the caseworker's written request accompanied by an affidavit, and prohibits open-records inspection of that information once removed.
