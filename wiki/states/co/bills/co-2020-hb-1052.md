## Summary

Colorado's HB 1052 extends the state's protected-persons privacy scheme to human-services-program employees who face credible threats. It prohibits any person from publicly posting or otherwise making such workers' personal information available on the internet when threat conditions are met, and establishes record-removal mechanisms to enforce that prohibition.
