## Summary

Colorado's Privacy Act (SB21-190) establishes comprehensive consumer data privacy rights for residents, applying to controllers doing business in the state that process personal data of 100,000 or more consumers annually, or 25,000 or more consumers when data sales generate revenue. Consumers gain rights to access, correct, delete, and port their data, plus opt-out of targeted advertising, data sales, and significant-decision profiling — including through a universal opt-out mechanism that controllers must recognize. Controllers must meet duties of transparency, data minimization, purpose limitation, and care, obtain consent before processing sensitive data, execute contracts with processors, and complete data protection assessments for high-risk processing activities. Enforcement rests exclusively with the Attorney General and district attorneys; the law took effect July 1, 2023, with no private right of action.
