## Summary

HB 1041 consolidates and expands Colorado's protected-persons privacy framework under C.R.S. 24-72-3xx, covering a broad class of at-risk individuals including judges, peace officers, and child-welfare and human-services workers. Covered persons may demand their personal information not be made public, and the law restricts publication with removal-on-request mechanisms backed by penalties.
