## Summary

HB 24-1058 amends the Colorado Privacy Act to classify biological data, explicitly including neural data, as sensitive personal data. Covered controllers must obtain opt-in consent before collecting or processing such data, making Colorado the first state to extend statutory privacy protections to neural and biological information. The amendment integrates neural data protection into Colorado's existing consumer privacy enforcement framework.
