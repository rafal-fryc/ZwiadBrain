## Summary

HB 1104 adds firefighters to Colorado's protected-persons internet-posting protections, prohibiting any person from making a qualifying firefighter's personal information publicly available online when threat conditions are present. The bill extends the same removal mechanisms already available to other protected classes under the state's scheme.
