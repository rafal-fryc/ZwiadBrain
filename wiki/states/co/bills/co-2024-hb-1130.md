## Summary

HB 1130 amends the Colorado Privacy Act to impose specific obligations on entities that collect or use biometric identifiers and biometric data, regardless of whether the entity meets the CPA's normal consumer-processing thresholds. Covered duties include obtaining consent before collection, maintaining a written retention and deletion policy, and prohibiting the sale of biometric identifiers; the law also limits employer collection of employees' biometric data.
