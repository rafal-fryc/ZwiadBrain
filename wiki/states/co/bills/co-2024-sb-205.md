## Summary

Colorado's SB 205, the Colorado AI Act, is the first comprehensive state AI law in the United States and takes effect February 1, 2026. It imposes duties on both developers and deployers of high-risk AI systems — defined as systems making or substantially influencing consequential decisions in domains including employment, housing, lending, education, healthcare, insurance, and legal services — to use reasonable care to protect against algorithmic discrimination. Developers must provide disclosure to deployers; deployers must implement risk-management programs and impact assessments and give consumers notice and appeal rights. Enforcement is exclusive to the Attorney General.
