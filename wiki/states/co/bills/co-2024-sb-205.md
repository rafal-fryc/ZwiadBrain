## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

Colorado SB 24-205, 'Consumer Protections for Artificial Intelligence,' was introduced on April 10, 2024, by Senate Majority Leader Robert Rodriguez (D-Denver) with House co-prime sponsors Representatives Manny Rutinel and Brianna Titone. Assigned to the Senate Judiciary Committee, the bill was heard on April 24, 2024, attracting substantial opposition from technology companies, trade associations, and small-business owners who testified that the compliance requirements were vague and would disadvantage Colorado businesses; civil-rights and labor advocates testified in support of the anti-discrimination protections. After adopting Amendment L.001 without objection, the committee advanced the bill along party lines, 3–2. Additional floor amendments were adopted before the Senate passed the bill on third reading on May 3, 2024, by a vote of 23–11. The House assigned the bill to its Committee on State, Civic, Military, and Veterans Affairs, which approved the bill with amendments L.006 and L.007 on May 4, 2024, and referred it to the full chamber; the House passed the bill on third reading on May 8, 2024, by 41–22, with a House floor amendment (H.001) failing 21–43. The Senate then concurred with the House amendments 35–0. Governor Jared Polis signed the measure into law on May 17, 2024, creating Colorado's—and the nation's—first comprehensive statutory framework for consumer protection against algorithmic discrimination by high-risk AI systems, while simultaneously issuing a formal letter expressing concern about the law's potential impact on innovation and urging the General Assembly to make significant improvements before the act's February 1, 2026 effective date. That effective date never took hold — the General Assembly first delayed it to June 30, 2026 through SB25B-004 (special session, signed August 28, 2025), and then SB26-189 (signed May 14, 2026) repealed and replaced SB 24-205's framework with a new one effective January 1, 2027—so SB 24-205 never took effect as written.
