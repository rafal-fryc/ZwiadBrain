## Summary

SB 41 amends the Colorado Privacy Act to establish heightened protections for minors' personal data online. Controllers offering services likely to be accessed by minors must exercise a duty of care and obtain consent before processing minors' data for targeted advertising, sale, or profiling; the law also requires design safeguards including restrictions on features designed to prolong engagement.
