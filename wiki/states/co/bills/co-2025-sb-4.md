## Summary

Colorado SB25B-004, enacted in a 2025 special legislative session, amends the state's Colorado AI Act to push back the compliance date for its algorithmic-discrimination provisions from February 1, 2026 to June 30, 2026. It preserves the underlying duties on developers and deployers of "high-risk" AI systems to use reasonable care against algorithmic discrimination, make required disclosures and documentation available, conduct impact assessments, and notify the attorney general of known risks or discovered discrimination, along with the deployer duty to disclose AI interactions to consumers. The bill does not otherwise change the substantive obligations, only the effective date by which developers and deployers must comply.
