  narrative: HB26-1139, "Use of Artificial Intelligence in Health Care," was introduced
    in the Colorado House on February 4, 2026 by Representatives Junie Joseph and
    Sheila Lieder, with Senators Lisa Cutter and Lindsey Daugherty as Senate sponsors.
    The bill regulates AI systems used in health care utilization review, requiring
    coverage determinations to rest on individual clinical circumstances, mandating
    human clinician review before any AI-informed denial, barring AI from being the
    sole basis for a coverage denial, requiring disclosure of AI use to state regulators,
    and prohibiting AI-conducted psychotherapy services. It was assigned to the House
    Health & Human Services Committee, which approved it with amendments on an 8-5
    vote on March 4, 2026; the House adopted further floor amendments on Second Reading
    (March 13) and passed the bill on Third Reading by a 47-15 vote on March 16, 2026.

    Labor, & Technology Committee, which advanced it unanimously (5-0) on May 5, 2026;
    the full Senate passed it on Third Reading by a 32-0 vote on May 11, 2026. Both
    chambers' presiding officers signed the enrolled act on May 28, 2026, and Governor
    Jared Polis signed it into law on June 2, 2026, enacting it as Chapter 325 of
    the 2026 Session Laws, with an effective date of January 1, 2027.

## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

HB26-1139, "Use of Artificial Intelligence in Health Care," was introduced in the Colorado House on February 4, 2026 by Representatives Junie Joseph and Sheila Lieder, with Senators Lisa Cutter and Lindsey Daugherty as Senate sponsors. The bill regulates AI systems used in health care utilization review, requiring coverage determinations to rest on individual clinical circumstances, mandating human clinician review before any AI-informed denial, barring AI from being the sole basis for a coverage denial, requiring disclosure of AI use to state regulators, and prohibiting AI-conducted psychotherapy services. It was assigned to the House Health & Human Services Committee, which approved it with amendments on an 8-5 vote on March 4, 2026; the House adopted further floor amendments on Second Reading (March 13) and passed the bill on Third Reading by a 47-15 vote on March 16, 2026. In the Senate, the bill was introduced May 4, 2026 and referred to the Business, Labor, & Technology Committee, which advanced it unanimously (5-0) on May 5, 2026; the full Senate passed it on Third Reading by a 32-0 vote on May 11, 2026. Both chambers' presiding officers signed the enrolled act on May 28, 2026, and Governor Jared Polis signed it into law on June 2, 2026, enacting it as Chapter 325 of the 2026 Session Laws, with an effective date of January 1, 2027.
