## Summary

Colorado HB 26-1263 (the Colorado Chatbot Safety Act), enacted July 1, 2026 and effective January 1, 2027, requires operators of conversational AI chatbot services to estimate or assess a user's age, disclose that users are interacting with AI rather than a human, and protect minors from sexually explicit content and from chatbot behavior simulating emotional dependence or romantic/sexual interest. Operators must also provide privacy and account-management tools for minor users, adopt suicide and self-harm response protocols that refer users to crisis resources, and are barred from representing chatbot outputs as equivalent to services from a licensed professional. The law is described as the first state statute specifically targeting AI chatbots and directs the Colorado Attorney General to conduct rulemaking on chatbot safety and automated decision-making.
