## Summary

Connecticut's SB 6, enacted as Public Act 22-15 (the Connecticut Data Privacy Act), establishes consumer rights over personal data — including access, correction, deletion, data portability, and opt-out of targeted advertising, sale of data, and certain profiling — and imposes duties on businesses that control or process the data of Connecticut residents, such as data minimization, purpose limitation, and mandatory data protection assessments for higher-risk processing. It applies to entities that conduct business in Connecticut (or target products/services to residents) and meet statutory thresholds based on the volume of consumer data processed or revenue derived from data sales, with exemptions for certain regulated industries and nonprofits. The Attorney General has exclusive enforcement authority, with an initial cure period for violations. The law took effect July 1, 2023.
