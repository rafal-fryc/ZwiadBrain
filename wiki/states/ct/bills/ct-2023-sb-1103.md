## Summary

Connecticut SB 1103 establishes a state Office of Artificial Intelligence, exempts air carriers from certain state data-privacy obligations, and bars controllers from processing a consumer's personal data for targeted advertising or selling it without consent where the controller has actual knowledge of, or willfully disregards, facts indicating the consumer is a minor. ⚠ Text extraction produced only a 'Chaptered' status marker, so this summary follows the official summary rather than the enacted text.
