## Summary

Connecticut SB 3 (2023) amends the Connecticut Data Privacy Act and adds online-safety duties. It regulates consumer health data — restricting its sale and sharing and prohibiting geofencing around facilities that provide in-person health care services to identify or track consumers seeking care — and adds requirements for the personal data of minors, including duties on social media platforms and online services around minors' accounts, default protections, and unsolicited contact. Enforcement runs through the Attorney General under the state's unfair trade practices framework.
