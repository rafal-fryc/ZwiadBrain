## Summary

Connecticut SB 1295 requires owners of social media platforms to build an online safety center into their platform and adopt a cyberbullying policy, expands the definition of 'heightened risk of harm to minors' under the state's data-privacy law to cover any reasonably foreseeable risk to minors' physical or mental health from data processing, and imposes added obligations on data controllers regarding minors. ⚠ Text extraction produced only a 'Chaptered' status marker, so this summary follows the official summary rather than the enacted text.
