## Summary

HB 5208, "An Act Concerning Data Security, Payroll Processing, Consumer Fraud Protection, Mortgage Loans and Employee Credit Rates," was signed by Gov. Lamont on May 27, 2026. Its core data-security provisions require certain entities and individuals handling customer information to adopt a written data-security program with reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, and require Department of Banking licensees and Connecticut banks and credit unions to notify the Department within three business days of a data-security incident. The bill also includes companion provisions establishing a payroll-processing study and a 19-member consumer-fraud working group, which are largely outside the bill's privacy/cyber focus.
