## Summary

HB 5222, an omnibus 2026 act also covering cannabis, fire inspections, and criminal mischief, contains first-in-nation AI and data-privacy provisions effective October 1, 2026 and enforced by the Attorney General under CUTPA (penalties up to $5,000 per willful violation). It establishes a novel AI-subscription consumer-protection framework regulating "subscription-based providers" that offer generative AI systems to consumers for a fee, imposing disclosure and cancellation-related obligations. Working alongside SB 4, it further amends the Connecticut Data Privacy Act's data-broker framework and surveillance-pricing disclosure rules, bans the sale of precise geolocation data, adds restrictions and disclosure requirements for facial-recognition technology used in physical security, and imposes compliance requirements on consumer genetic-testing companies.
