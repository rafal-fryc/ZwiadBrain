## Summary

Connecticut HB 5312 creates a private right of action for victims of the unlawful dissemination of synthetically created (deepfake) intimate images and a separate civil action for the Attorney General, allowing damages to be sought from an operator of an electronic platform that fails to take down such an image after knowing or having reason to know its dissemination was unlawful. ⚠ Text extraction produced only a 'Chaptered' status marker, so this summary follows the official summary rather than the enacted text.
