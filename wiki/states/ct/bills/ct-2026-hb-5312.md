## Summary

Connecticut HB 5312 (Public Act 26-55), effective October 1, 2026, lets a person harmed by the unlawful dissemination of a synthetically created intimate image sue in Superior Court for an injunction and economic, noneconomic and punitive damages. The suit lies against an individual who knowingly disseminates the image with intent to harm. It must be filed within two years of discovering the dissemination, and the court may protect the plaintiff's identity. A separate section requires a covered platform (as described in 47 USC 223a) to run a notice-and-removal process and to remove a reported image, and make reasonable efforts to remove copies, within 48 hours of a valid request from the harmed individual or the Attorney General. On the Attorney General's petition, a platform that violates this can face civil penalties of up to $25,000 per day, and platforms acting in good faith are shielded from liability for removals.
