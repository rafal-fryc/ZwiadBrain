## Summary

Public Act 26-64 (SB 4) is an omnibus amendment to the Connecticut Data Privacy Act, signed by Gov. Lamont on May 27, 2026. It creates a data broker registry with a single consumer mechanism to request deletion across all registered brokers, bans the sale of Connecticut residents' precise geolocation data, requires consumer consent before repurposing personal data for uses incompatible with the original disclosed purpose, and adds disclosure requirements for data-driven "surveillance pricing" and new compliance obligations for direct-to-consumer genetic testing companies. It also narrows the CTDPA's "publicly available information" exemption to exclude genetic data, intimate images, and data where consumers had a reasonable expectation of privacy, with enforcement reserved exclusively to the Connecticut Attorney General. Most provisions take effect October 1, 2026, with data-broker registration effective January 1, 2027 and surveillance-pricing rules effective July 1, 2027.
