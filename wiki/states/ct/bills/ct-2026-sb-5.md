## Summary

SB 5, the Connecticut Online Safety Act, is a 39-section AI and youth-online-safety law signed in 2026 and effective October 1, 2026, ranking among the nation's most comprehensive AI/online-safety statutes. It requires social media companies to verify users' ages and obtain parental or guardian consent before minors can access addictive algorithmic feeds, sets default protections for minors (limits on notifications, parental controls, warning labels, and restrictions on addictive feeds) that can only be loosened with parental consent, and mandates annual disclosure and usage-statistics reporting. For users under 18, it prohibits access to AI companion systems where it is reasonably foreseeable the system could encourage self-harm, violence, disordered eating, unlawful drug or alcohol use, romantic or sexually explicit interaction, or otherwise use engagement-maximizing techniques that override safety, alongside additional provisions covering AI companions and employment-related AI.
