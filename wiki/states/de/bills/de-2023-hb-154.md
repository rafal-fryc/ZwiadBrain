## Summary

Delaware's HB 154 enacts the Delaware Personal Data Privacy Act (Title 6, ch. 12D), applying to persons doing business in the state that process the personal data of at least 35,000 consumers, or 10,000 consumers while deriving more than 20% of gross revenue from data sales. It gives consumers rights to access, correct, delete, and port their data and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and significant-effect profiling, requires opt-in consent for processing sensitive data (including a known child's data), and imposes controller/processor duties such as data minimization, security safeguards, privacy notices, and data protection assessments for high-risk processing, enforced by the state Department of Justice. It takes effect January 1, 2025.

## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

House Bill 154, the Delaware Personal Data Privacy Act, was introduced by Rep. Krista Griffith, with Rep. Paul Baumbach, Sen. Sherry Dorsey Walker, and Sen. Kerri Harris among the additional sponsors, and assigned to the House Technology & Telecommunications Committee on May 12, 2023. The committee reported the bill out on May 16, 2023, and it was then referred to House Appropriations, which reported it out on June 6, 2023, after the Department of Justice's fiscal note estimated the recurring costs of standing up enforcement staff within the Consumer Protection Fund. On June 8, 2023, the House adopted Amendments HA 1 and HA 4 by voice vote and passed the bill 33-5 (1 not voting, 2 absent), sending it to the Senate Banking, Business, Insurance & Technology Committee the same day. In the Senate, Senate Amendment SA 1 was placed on June 27, 2023, and the bill moved through committee (reported out June 28) and Senate Finance (reported out June 29) before SA 1 was adopted and the bill passed the Senate 15-5 on June 29, 2023. The House concurred in the Senate amendment, passing the bill 37-3 on June 30, 2023, completing legislative action. Governor John Carney signed the Delaware Personal Data Privacy Act into law on September 11, 2023, with an effective date of January 1, 2025. As enacted, the law amends Title 6 of the Delaware Code to impose controller and processor duties on entities processing the personal data of 35,000 or more Delaware consumers, grants consumers rights to access, correct, delete, and port their personal data, requires opt-outs for targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and profiling, and vests enforcement authority in the Delaware Department of Justice.
