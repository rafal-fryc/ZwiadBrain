## Summary

Delaware's HB 154 enacts the Delaware Personal Data Privacy Act (Title 6, ch. 12D), applying to persons doing business in the state that process the personal data of at least 35,000 consumers, or 10,000 consumers while deriving more than 20% of gross revenue from data sales. It gives consumers rights to access, correct, delete, and port their data and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and significant-effect profiling, requires opt-in consent for processing sensitive data (including a known child's data), and imposes controller/processor duties such as data minimization, security safeguards, privacy notices, and data protection assessments for high-risk processing, enforced by the state Department of Justice. It takes effect January 1, 2025.
