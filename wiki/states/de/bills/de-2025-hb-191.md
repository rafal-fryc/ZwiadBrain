## Summary

Delaware HB 191 amends Title 24 of the Delaware Code to bar any 'nonhuman entity, including an agent powered by artificial intelligence,' from being licensed or certified as a registered nurse, advanced practice registered nurse, licensed practical nurse, clinical nurse specialist, physician, or physician assistant, and from using the associated professional titles or abbreviations such as 'RN', 'APRN', 'MD', 'DO', 'PA' or 'Dr.'
