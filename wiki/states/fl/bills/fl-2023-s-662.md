## Summary

Florida's SB 662 (2023) enacts the Student Online Personal Information Protection Act, prohibiting operators of websites, online services, or apps used primarily for K-12 school purposes from knowingly using students' covered information for targeted advertising, building non-educational profiles, or selling/sharing/disclosing that data except in limited circumstances (legal compliance, school purposes, safety, or contractually restricted vendors). Operators must limit data collection to what's reasonably necessary, maintain reasonable security, and delete covered information within 90 days after a student leaves the district absent parental consent to retain it; the Department of Legal Affairs is the sole enforcer under the state's deceptive trade practices act. It took effect July 1, 2023.
