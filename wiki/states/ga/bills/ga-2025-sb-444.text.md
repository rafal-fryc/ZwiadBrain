## AS PASSED

## A BILL TO BE ENTITLED

## AN ACT

To amend Chapter 46 of Title 33 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated, relating to 1 certification of private review agents, so as to provide that certain decisions with regard to 2 the provision of insurance coverage for healthcare services shall not be based solely on 3 artificial intelligence systems, artificial intelligence, or other software tools; to provide for 4 definitions;  to  provide  for  related  matters;  to  provide  for  an  effective  date;  to  repeal 5 conflicting laws; and for other purposes. 6
