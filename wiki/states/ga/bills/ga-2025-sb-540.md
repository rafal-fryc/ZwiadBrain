## Summary

Georgia SB 540 regulates operators of AI companion chatbots. Operators must clearly disclose that the user is interacting with AI at the start of each session and at least every three hours (hourly for known or likely minor users); provide age-appropriate protections for minors, barring sexual content, romantic role-play, and isolation-encouraging or engagement-manipulating tactics; maintain a suicide and self-harm crisis-response protocol with a published summary and annual aggregate reporting; offer parental control tools; and apply commercially reasonable age assurance before granting access to sexually explicit generative features. The Attorney General enforces the law civilly, with penalties up to $10,000 per knowing violation plus damages and injunctive relief, and a 30-day cure option for first-time non-egregious violations. It takes effect July 1, 2027.
