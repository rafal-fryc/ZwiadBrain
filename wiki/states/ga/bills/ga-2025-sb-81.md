## Summary

Georgia's SB 81 amends the Motor Vehicle Franchise Practices Act to protect consumer and dealer data in dealer-manufacturer relationships. Franchisors, manufacturers, distributors, and their affiliates may not require access to a dealer's data management system; direct access needs the dealer's express written consent in a separate document, withdrawable on 30 days' written notice. They must also give dealers a written list of consumer data shared in the prior six months on request and indemnify dealers for violations. A new Code section 10-1-633 makes it unlawful for third parties such as dealer data systems vendors and authorized integrators to access, share, sell, or copy protected dealer data without the dealer's prior express written consent or to impose data access overcharges or unreasonable integration restrictions. It requires vendors to offer secure open APIs, allows dealers to terminate vendor contracts on no more than 90 days' notice, requires secure data transition and indemnification, and protects dealers' right to monitor, audit, and back up their data. The bill also revises legislative findings to cover recall service, adds recall work to dealer obligations, and amends Code Section 40-2-39 on new motor vehicle dealer activities; it took effect July 1, 2025.
