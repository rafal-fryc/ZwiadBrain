## Summary

Georgia's SB 81 amends the state's Motor Vehicle Franchise Practices Act to add data-rights and security protections for franchise dealers within the manufacturer-dealer relationship. It requires manufacturers, distributors, and third-party vendors to obtain a dealer's prior written consent before accessing consumer or dealer data stored in dealer management systems, mandates secure open APIs for data integration, prohibits unreasonable charges for data access, and ensures dealers can monitor and audit who accesses their data; it also expands the Act's competition/consumer-protection findings and adds 'recall service' to the services covered franchise dealers perform. The law binds manufacturers, distributors, and third-party data vendors and took effect July 1, 2025.
