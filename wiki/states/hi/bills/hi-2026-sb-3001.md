## Summary

Hawaii SB 3001, the Artificial Intelligence Disclosure and Safety Act, requires operators of AI companions to disclose that the user is interacting with AI (persistently, or at session start and hourly for known minors); adopt a protocol that refers users expressing suicidal ideation or self-harm to crisis resources; and take reasonable measures to stop companions from claiming to be human or licensed mental-health providers, from using unpredictable reward mechanisms with minors, and from producing sexual content involving minors. From January 1, 2028 operators must file annual reports with the Department of Health's Behavioral Health Administration on crisis referrals and safety protocols. Violations are unfair or deceptive trade practices enforced by the state; the act creates no private right of action.
