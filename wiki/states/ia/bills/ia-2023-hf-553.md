## Summary

Iowa House File 553 creates a new Iowa Code chapter (554G) establishing an affirmative defense against tort claims for a covered entity (any business, including financial institutions, that accesses, receives, stores, maintains, communicates, or processes personal or restricted information) that maintains a written cybersecurity program reasonably conforming to a recognized framework such as the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, NIST SP 800-171 or 800-53/53A, FedRAMP, CIS Critical Security Controls, ISO/IEC 27000, or applicable sector-specific regimes like HIPAA security rules, Gramm-Leach-Bliley Title V, FISMA, or PCI DSS. The defense applies to tort suits alleging that a failure to implement reasonable information-security controls caused a data breach of personal or restricted information, but the chapter creates no private right of action. Effective July 1, 2023.
