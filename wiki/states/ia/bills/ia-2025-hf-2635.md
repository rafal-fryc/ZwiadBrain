## Summary

Iowa HF 2635 regulates health carriers and utilization review organizations. AI-based algorithms may conduct an initial review of prior-authorization requests, but AI may not be the sole basis for denying, delaying, or downgrading a medical-necessity determination. The act also sets provider audit and appeal timelines, requires prior-authorization denials and downgrades to be made or reviewed by a qualified physician or clinical peer with a written explanation, exempts certain cancer screenings and emergency care from prior authorization, and revises the state's certificate-of-need cost thresholds. The Commissioner of Insurance enforces it; effective July 1, 2026.
