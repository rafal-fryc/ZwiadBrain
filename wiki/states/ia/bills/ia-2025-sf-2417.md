## Summary

Iowa SF 2417 sets requirements for conversational AI services offered to the public. Operators must disclose to minor users that they are interacting with AI; must not use unpredictable engagement-reward mechanisms or direct sexual or romance-simulating content at minors; must provide privacy and account-management tools; must maintain a suicide and self-harm crisis-referral protocol; and must not represent that the service provides licensed mental-health care. The Attorney General enforces the act with civil penalties of $1,000 per violation (capped at $500,000 per operator) or actual damages, with no private right of action. Effective July 1, 2026, applicable to conduct on or after July 1, 2027.
