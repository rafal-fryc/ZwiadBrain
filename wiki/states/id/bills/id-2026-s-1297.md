## Summary

Idaho SB 1297, the Conversational AI Safety Act, requires operators of public-facing conversational AI services to disclose that the user is interacting with AI where a reasonable person could otherwise be misled, maintain a suicide-ideation crisis-referral protocol, and refrain from claiming to provide licensed mental or behavioral health care. For minor account holders, operators must give more frequent AI disclosures and must avoid unpredictable engagement rewards, sexual content, and human-like romantic or dependency-simulating statements, and must offer privacy and account tools to the minor or, for users under 13, to a parent. The Attorney General enforces the act with civil penalties of $1,000 per violation (capped at $500,000 per operator) or actual damages, whichever is greater; no private right of action. Effective July 1, 2027.
