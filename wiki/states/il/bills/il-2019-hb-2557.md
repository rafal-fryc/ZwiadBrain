## Summary

Illinois's Artificial Intelligence Video Interview Act, effective January 1, 2020, requires employers using AI to analyze applicant video interviews to notify applicants in advance, explain the AI technology and the characteristics it evaluates, and obtain consent before analysis. Employers must restrict access to videos to those necessary for candidate evaluation and must destroy recordings within 30 days upon an applicant's request.
