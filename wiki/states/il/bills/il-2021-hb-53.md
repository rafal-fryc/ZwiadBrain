## Summary

This amendment to the Artificial Intelligence Video Interview Act adds a racial equity reporting obligation for employers who rely solely on AI analysis to screen applicants for in-person interviews. Such employers must collect race and ethnicity demographic data from applicants and submit annual reports to the state, which analyzes the data for evidence of racial bias.
