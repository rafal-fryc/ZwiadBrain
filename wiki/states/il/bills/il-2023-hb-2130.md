## Summary

The Illinois Insurance Data Security Law, based on the NAIC model act, requires insurance licensees to develop and maintain a written information security program grounded in a risk assessment of their data environment. Licensees must investigate cybersecurity events and notify the Director of Insurance of qualifying incidents within prescribed timeframes.
