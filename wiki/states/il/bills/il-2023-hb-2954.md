## Summary

The Civil Liability for Doxing Act establishes a civil cause of action against any person who intentionally publishes another individual's personally identifiable information with the intent that it be used to harm or harass, where that publication causes actual harm. Victims may seek damages and injunctive relief against the person responsible for the disclosure.
