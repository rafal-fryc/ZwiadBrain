## Summary

Effective January 1, 2026, this amendment to the Illinois Human Rights Act prohibits employers from using artificial intelligence that produces a discriminatory effect in employment decisions including recruitment, hiring, promotion, discipline, and discharge. Employers are also barred from using zip codes as proxies for protected classes and must notify employees and applicants whenever AI is used in decisions affecting their employment.
