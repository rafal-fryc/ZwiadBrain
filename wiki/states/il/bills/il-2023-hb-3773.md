## Summary

Reinserts the provisions of the engrossed bill with the following changes. Further amends the Employment Article of the Illinois Human Rights Act. Provides that it is a civil rights violation: (1) with respect to recruitment, hiring, promotion, renewal of employment, selection for training or apprenticeship, discharge, discipline, tenure, or the terms, privileges, or conditions of employment, for an employer to use artificial intelligence that has the effect of subjecting employees to discrimination on the basis of protected classes identified under the Article or to use zip codes as a proxy for protected classes identified under the Article; and (2) for an employer to fail to provide notice to an employee that the employer is using artificial intelligence. Defines "artificial intelligence" and "generative artificial intelligence". Removes the amendatory changes to the Consumer Fraud and Deceptive Business Practices Act. Makes other changes. Adds a January 1, 2026 effective date.

## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

HB 3773 was introduced on February 17, 2023, by Rep. Jaime M. Andrade Jr. in the Illinois House of Representatives (103rd General Assembly) as a measure to prohibit employers from using predictive data analytics to consider an applicant's race or ZIP code in employment decisions. Through successive committee amendments, the bill's scope expanded substantially. The House Labor and Commerce Committee approved an amended version 27-0-0 on April 3, 2024, and endorsed House Floor Amendment No. 3 by a 29-0-0 recommendation on April 18; the full House passed the bill on third reading 106-0-1 on April 19, 2024. In the Senate, the Judiciary Committee approved the bill on a closer 6-3-0 vote on May 8, 2024, reflecting some opposition. Sen. Javier L. Cervantes filed Senate Floor Amendment No. 3 on May 14, 2024, substantially replacing the original 'predictive data analytics' framework with broader prohibitions on employer use of artificial intelligence that produces a discriminatory effect, adding a new employer notice requirement, definitions for artificial intelligence and generative artificial intelligence, expanded harassment protections, language-rights provisions, and reinforced religious accommodation duties; the Senate approved that amendment and passed the bill 57-0-0 on May 15, 2024. The House Labor and Commerce Committee recommended concurrence 28-0-0 on May 20, and the full House concurred in the Senate amendments 100-15-0 on May 24, 2024. Governor JB Pritzker signed the bill into law as Public Act 103-0804 on August 9, 2024; the Act takes effect January 1, 2026, and directs the Illinois Department of Human Rights to adopt implementing regulations.
