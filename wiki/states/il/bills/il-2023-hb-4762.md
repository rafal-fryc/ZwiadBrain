## Summary

Illinois HB 4762, the Digital Voice and Likeness Protection Act, makes a provision in an agreement for personal or professional services unenforceable where it allows the creation and use of a digital replica of an individual's voice or likeness in place of work the individual would otherwise have performed in person, unless the agreement reasonably specifies the intended uses and the individual was represented by counsel or a labor union. ⚠ Text extraction produced no body text and the official LegiScan summary describes an amendment stage rather than the enacted law, so this summary is limited to the act's established subject matter and should be verified against the enacted text.
