## Summary

Amends the Right of Publicity Act. Grants additional enforcement rights and remedies to recording artists. Provides for the liability of any person who materially contributes to, induces, or otherwise facilitates a violation of a specified provision of the Act by another party after having reason to know that the other party is in violation. Defines "artificial intelligence" and "generative artificial intelligence". Changes the definition of "commercial purpose" and "identity".

## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

HB 4875, the "Publicity Act - Use of AI" bill, was filed with the House Clerk on February 6, 2024 by lead sponsor Rep. Jennifer Gong-Gershowitz, with numerous House cosponsors including Reps. Mary Edly-Allen, Daniel Didech, Jawaharial Williams, Sharon Chung, Jehan Gordon-Booth, Terra Costa Howard, Jenn Ladisch Douglass, Kambium Buckner, and Harry Benton. The bill amends the Illinois Right of Publicity Act (765 ILCS 1075) to regulate AI-generated "digital replicas" of an individual's voice or likeness, requiring consent for their commercial use and voiding contracts that substitute a digital replica for a performer's own work unless the performer is represented by counsel or a union. It cleared the House Judiciary - Civil Committee on March 13, 2024 (11-3) and passed House Third Reading on April 18, 2024 by a vote of 79-24. In the Senate, sponsored by Sen. Mary Edly-Allen, it passed the Judiciary Committee on May 8, 2024 (6-3), and on May 17, 2024 the Senate adopted Senate Floor Amendment No. 3 - which added the digital-replica definitions, safe-harbor provisions for platforms, and an extended 50-year post-mortem protection period - before passing Third Reading unanimously, 58-0. The House concurred in the Senate amendment on May 24, 2024 by a 112-0 vote, sending the bill to the Governor. Governor J.B. Pritzker approved the bill on August 9, 2024, and it was enacted as Public Act 103-0836, taking effect January 1, 2025.
