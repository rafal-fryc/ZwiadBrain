## Summary

This amendment to the Biometric Information Privacy Act, enacted in response to Cothron v. White Castle, limits statutory damages by providing that repeated collection or disclosure of the same individual's biometric data in the same manner constitutes a single violation, capping recovery at one violation per person rather than one per scan or transaction. The amendment also clarifies that written consent required under BIPA may be satisfied through an electronic signature.
