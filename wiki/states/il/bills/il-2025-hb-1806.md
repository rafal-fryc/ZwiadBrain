## Summary

Illinois' Wellness and Oversight for Psychological Resources Act (HB 1806, enacted as P.A. 104-054) bars any individual, corporation, or entity — including AI systems offered over the Internet — from providing, advertising, or offering therapy or psychotherapy services to the public unless delivered by a state-licensed mental health professional (e.g., licensed clinical psychologists, social workers, counselors, marriage and family therapists, certain drug counselors and psychiatric nurses). Licensed professionals may use AI only for defined "administrative support" or "supplementary support" tasks (such as record-keeping or organizing referrals) while retaining full responsibility for all AI-assisted interactions and outputs, and AI recording/transcription of therapy sessions requires prior written disclosure and patient consent; AI may never make independent therapeutic decisions, directly engage in therapeutic communication with clients, generate treatment plans without professional review, or detect emotions/mental states. The Department of Financial and Professional Regulation enforces the Act through civil penalties of up to $10,000 per violation after a hearing, and the law exempts religious counseling, peer support, and public self-help materials; it took effect immediately upon becoming law.
