## Summary

Illinois HB 3178 (enacted as P.A. 104-282) amends the state's Right of Publicity Act's digital-replica provisions (originally added by P.A. 103-830), which govern licensing of an individual's "digital replica" — an AI- or computer-generated simulation of a person's voice or likeness. As enacted, it clarifies that a licensing agreement authorizing use of an individual's digital replica rights is not rendered unenforceable merely because it lacks a reasonably specific description of the replica's intended uses, so long as the actual use is consistent with the contract's terms and the character of the underlying recorded photography or sound track; it also preserves that a licensor may act on an individual's behalf via written agreement or through a labor union collective bargaining agreement expressly covering digital-replica uses. The law took effect January 1, 2026.
