## Summary

Illinois SB 3114 (Public Act 104-0568), the Transparency in Downcoding Act, bars health care payors from using any algorithm or automated process that bypasses a billing provider's submitted information to unilaterally downcode a claim, and prohibits downcoding based solely on diagnosis codes. Every downcoding decision and dispute must be made or reviewed by a natural person applying current AMA CPT guidelines. Payors must notify providers of downcoding with specific reasons via standard remark and adjustment codes and provide a dispute process with at least a 90-day filing window. The Department of Insurance enforces the act (the Department of Healthcare and Family Services for Medicaid managed care); it preempts local regulation of downcoding and takes effect January 1, 2028.
