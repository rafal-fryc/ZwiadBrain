## Summary

Illinois' AI Safety Measures Act (SB 315, enacted as P.A. 104-538) regulates "large frontier developers" — companies with affiliated annual gross revenues over $500 million that train or deploy "frontier models" (foundation models trained with more than 10^26 floating-point operations). Beginning January 1, 2028, covered developers must publish and annually update a "frontier AI framework" describing how they assess and mitigate "catastrophic risk" (e.g., CBRN weapon assistance, autonomous cyberattacks or violent crime, loss of model control), publish transparency reports before deploying new or materially modified frontier models, and retain independent third-party auditors to verify compliance. Frontier developers of any size must report "critical safety incidents" to a new state Agency and the Attorney General within 72 hours (24 hours for imminent death/injury risk), file annual disclosure statements and pay fees starting January 1, 2027, and provide whistleblower protections for employees who report safety concerns; the Attorney General may bring civil actions with penalties up to $1,000,000 for a first violation and $3,000,000 for subsequent violations, and the Act creates no private right of action.
