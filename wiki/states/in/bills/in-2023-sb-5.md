## Summary

Indiana's SB 5 (2023) enacts the Indiana Consumer Data Protection Act, a comprehensive privacy law modeled on Virginia's framework that applies to businesses meeting certain data-volume or revenue thresholds. It gives Indiana consumers rights to access, correct, delete, and obtain a portable copy of their personal data and to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and certain profiling, while requiring controllers to limit data collection, secure personal data, and obtain consent before processing sensitive data. The Attorney General has exclusive enforcement authority (no private right of action), and the law took effect January 1, 2026.
