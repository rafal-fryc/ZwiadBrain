## Summary

Indiana SB 17 (2024) requires operators of adult-oriented websites that display material harmful to minors to use a reasonable age-verification method to prevent minors from accessing the site. Beyond the access rule, it imposes two company-facing data-privacy duties on any person conducting the age verification: it prohibits retaining the identifying information of an individual who seeks access (a data-minimization mandate), and it requires operators to use commercially reasonable methods to secure all information collected and transmitted. Enforcement runs through a private right of action (parents/guardians of a harmed minor, and individuals whose identifying information is unlawfully retained, may seek damages, injunctive relief, and attorney's fees) and the Attorney General, who may seek an injunction or a civil penalty of up to $250,000.
