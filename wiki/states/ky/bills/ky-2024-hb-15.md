## Summary

Kentucky's HB 15 (2024) enacts the Kentucky Consumer Data Protection Act, applying to businesses that annually control or process the personal data of at least 100,000 Kentucky consumers, or 25,000 consumers while deriving over 50% of revenue from data sales. It grants consumers rights to access, correct, delete, and port personal data and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale of data, and significant-effect profiling, requires opt-in consent for processing sensitive data, and mandates data protection assessments for higher-risk processing (applicable to activities generated on or after June 1, 2026). The Attorney General has exclusive enforcement authority with a 30-day cure period and penalties up to $7,500 per violation; the Act was signed April 4, 2024, and takes effect January 1, 2026.
