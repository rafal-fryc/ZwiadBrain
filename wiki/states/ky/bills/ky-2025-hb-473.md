## Summary

Kentucky HB 473 (2025) amends the Kentucky Consumer Data Protection Act (KRS 367.3611 et seq.). It adds an exemption for health data covered by HIPAA and requires controllers to conduct data protection assessments for profiling that presents a reasonably foreseeable risk of unlawful disparate impact on consumers, alongside the Act's other assessment triggers.
