## Summary

Kentucky HB 692 amends the Kentucky Consumer Data Protection Act to define 'automatic content recognition' (ACR) data — content-viewing history collected via smart TVs or smart monitors that identify displayed audio and video in real time — and 'smart monitor', and adds ACR data alongside biometric data to the statute's definition of sensitive data, so controllers may not collect it without consumer consent. Signed April 13, 2026; the amendments take effect July 1, 2027.
