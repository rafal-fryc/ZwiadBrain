## Summary

Louisiana HB 521 (Act 224, 2024) amends R.S. 22:918 to expand the state's genetic-information nondiscrimination protections for life and long-term care insurers. It bars insurers from canceling, limiting, refusing to renew, denying coverage, or increasing premium rates on an existing policy based on an individual's or family member's genetic research participation or receipt of genetic services (previously the statute only barred cancellation/nonrenewal, not limiting coverage or raising rates). The existing prohibition on using genetic research/testing participation in underwriting, eligibility, or premium decisions is retained, with the exception that insurers may still consider genetic test results that are in the individual's medical record or voluntarily submitted by the individual. The bill was signed into law as Act 224.
