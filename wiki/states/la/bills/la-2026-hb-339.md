## Summary

Louisiana HB 339 (Act 543) amends R.S. 44:11.2, the state's limited-access-to-personal-information law for "protected individuals," by adding current law enforcement officers and current or retired administrative law judges to the list of covered persons. It defines "publish" as publicly posting or displaying a protected individual's personal information on the internet, and prohibits any person from publishing that information after the individual has submitted a removal request under the statute, unless the publisher has a legitimate political, economic, or societal interest, or does so with intent to intimidate or harass. The law binds private third-party publishers as well as public bodies, allowing a protected individual (or, for judges, the judicial administrator's office, or for certain individuals the Louisiana District Attorneys Association) to sue for mandamus, attorney fees, and, against third parties, damages for violations; it repeals prior subsections (G) and (I). Signed into law as Act 543.
