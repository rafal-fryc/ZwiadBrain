## Summary

Maryland's HB 567 (2024) enacts the Maryland Online Data Privacy Act, a comprehensive consumer privacy law covering businesses that process Marylanders' personal data, notable for imposing stricter data-minimization requirements than most state privacy laws — limiting collection, use, and retention of personal data to what is reasonably necessary for the specific product or service requested, rather than merely what is disclosed. It grants consumers rights to access, correct, delete, and port their data and to opt out of targeted advertising and sale, bars sale of sensitive data outright in most cases, and imposes heightened limits on processing minors' data, with the Attorney General as sole enforcer. The law becomes effective October 1, 2025, with enforcement following in 2025.
