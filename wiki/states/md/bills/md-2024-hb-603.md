## Summary

Maryland's HB 603 (2024), the Maryland Kids Code, requires online products and services likely to be accessed by children to complete data protection impact assessments before launch and to configure default privacy settings, data collection, and geolocation features to the most protective level for minors. It restricts profiling, targeted advertising, and dark-pattern design practices directed at children and limits collection and use of children's personal data to what is necessary for the service provided, with enforcement by the Attorney General.
