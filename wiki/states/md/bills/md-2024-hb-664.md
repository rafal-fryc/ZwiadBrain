## Summary

Maryland's HB 664, the Judge Andrew F. Wilkinson Judicial Security Act (2024), lets Maryland judges and other protected court personnel request that government agencies and private persons, publishers, and data brokers refrain from publicly posting their home address and other personal information online, and requires removal of such information already posted, modeled on New Jersey's Daniel's Law. The law creates a mechanism for covered individuals to send removal requests enforceable against noncompliant private parties, not merely government recordkeepers.
