## Summary

Maryland's HB 664 (Chapter 414), the Judge Andrew F. Wilkinson Judicial Security Act, lets protected individuals, or the Administrative Office of the Courts' Office of Information Privacy on their behalf, demand that governmental entities and private persons not publish, or remove from existing publication, their personal information (such as home address, phone numbers, vehicle plate, children's names, schools, and places of worship). Protected individuals are current and retired Maryland judges, federal judges and magistrates domiciled in Maryland, circuit court magistrates, District Court commissioners, and household family members. A private recipient must remove the information within 72 hours and notify the requester, and violations support declaratory and injunctive relief, damages, attorney's fees, and punitive damages for willful refusal. The Act also makes it a misdemeanor (up to 18 months and/or a $5,000 fine) to knowingly publish a protected individual's information when it poses an imminent and serious threat and results in assault, harassment, trespass, or malicious destruction of property. It creates a Judicial Address Confidentiality Program with substitute addresses and shielded real property records, and a Task Force to Ensure the Safety of Judicial Facilities.
