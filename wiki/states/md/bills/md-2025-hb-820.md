## Summary

Maryland HB 820 (2025) governs how artificial intelligence may be used in health-insurance utilization review. It binds carriers, pharmacy benefits managers, and private review agents, setting requirements and limits on the use of AI and algorithmic tools in utilization review determinations rather than leaving such decisions to automated systems alone. AI regulation is the operative purpose of the act, not an incidental mention in a health bill.
