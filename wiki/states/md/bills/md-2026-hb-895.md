## Summary

Maryland HB 895, the Protection From Predatory Pricing Act, bars food retailers (grocery stores of at least 15,000 square feet) and third-party food delivery services from using dynamic pricing or 'surveillance personal data' (including AI-driven models) to charge a specific consumer or group a higher price for sales-tax-exempt food, and from using protected-class data to withhold goods, services, or benefits. Separately, any merchant that sets a price using dynamic pricing or personal data must display a clear disclosure that the price was set by an algorithm or personal data. Violations are enforced as unfair, abusive, or deceptive trade practices under the Maryland Consumer Protection Act, with a 45-day cure notice for the pricing provisions and no private right of action for those sections. Took effect October 1, 2026 as an emergency measure.
