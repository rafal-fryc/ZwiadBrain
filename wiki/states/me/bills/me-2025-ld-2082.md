## Summary

Maine's LD 2082 (Public Law Chapter 687), sponsored by Rep. Amy Kuhn, regulates the use of artificial intelligence in mental health service delivery by barring AI systems from making independent therapeutic decisions, engaging directly in therapeutic communication with clients, or generating treatment plans or recommendations without review by a licensed clinician. AI tools may be used only in administrative or supplementary roles, with the licensed practitioner retaining full responsibility for care. It was approved by the Governor on April 13, 2026, and takes effect July 29, 2026.
