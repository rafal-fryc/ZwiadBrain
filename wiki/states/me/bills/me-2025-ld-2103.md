## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

LD 2103, "An Act Requiring Hospitals to Adopt Cybersecurity Plans," was introduced by Rep. Julia McCabe of Lewiston in the Maine House during the 132nd Legislature's Second Regular Session and referred to the Joint Standing Committee on Health and Human Services on January 7, 2026. The committee held a work session and voted on March 11, 2026, reporting the bill out on March 30, 2026 with an "Ought to Pass as Amended" recommendation carried in Committee Amendment C-A (H-937), which the fiscal-note record shows added no net fiscal impact at that stage beyond the original bill's projected costs for a new Social Services Program Manager position within the Division of Licensing and Certification. The House passed the bill on April 2, 2026, and the Senate passed it on April 6, 2026; the official record shows no roll-call tally for these floor actions. Governor Janet Mills signed the bill into law on April 13, 2026, and it was designated Public Law 2025, Chapter 668. The enacted law requires every hospital licensed in Maine to adopt and annually review a cybersecurity plan aligned with federal best practices and HIPAA, developed with a hospital-convened worker/labor-union working group and subject to annual independent audit, with the cybersecurity provisions taking effect January 1, 2027.
