## Summary

Maine's LD 2103 requires every hospital licensed under state law to adopt and annually review a cybersecurity plan consistent with federal cybersecurity best practices (CISA, NIST) and HIPAA, covering intrusion notification to regulators, law enforcement, patients and media, backup communication and patient-complaint procedures during system disruptions, continuity-of-care and service-triage protocols, annual staff cybersecurity training, and annual downtime testing. The plan must be developed with input from a hospital-convened worker/labor-union working group and undergo an annual independent cybersecurity audit, with a summary reported to the state health department on request. The cybersecurity provisions take effect January 1, 2027.
