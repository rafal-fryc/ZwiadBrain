## Summary

Minnesota's HF 4757 (2024), enacted as part of a larger commerce/cannabis omnibus bill, establishes the Minnesota Consumer Data Privacy Act, a comprehensive privacy law imposing controller and processor duties on businesses handling Minnesotans' personal data. It grants consumers rights to access, correct, delete, and port their data, opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and profiling, and — notably beyond most state privacy laws — a right to question the result of profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects and request a review of the decision. The Act takes effect July 31, 2025.
