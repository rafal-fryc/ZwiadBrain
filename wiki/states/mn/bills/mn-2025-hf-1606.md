## Summary

Minnesota HF 1606 makes it unlawful for a person who owns or controls a website, app, or other service to allow users to 'nudify' an image or video — to use technology to alter or generate an image so it realistically depicts an identifiable person's intimate parts not shown in the original — or to perform nudification on a user's behalf, and bans advertising such services. An exemption applies where genuine human technical or artistic skill is required. Depicted individuals may sue for up to triple compensatory damages, punitive damages, and attorney's fees, and the Attorney General may impose civil penalties up to $500,000 per violation, with collected penalties funding crime-victim services. Took effect August 1, 2026.
