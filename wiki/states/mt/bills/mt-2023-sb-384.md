## Summary

Montana SB 384 (2023) enacts the Montana Consumer Data Privacy Act, the state's comprehensive consumer privacy law, effective October 1, 2024. It applies to persons conducting business in Montana or targeting products or services to Montana residents who either control or process the personal data of at least 50,000 consumers (excluding data processed solely to complete a payment transaction), or control or process the personal data of at least 25,000 consumers and derive more than a specified share of gross revenue from selling personal data. It grants consumer rights over personal data, imposes controller and processor duties, requires data protection assessments, and defines biometric data. The Attorney General has exclusive enforcement authority and must issue a notice of violation first, with a 60-day cure period; there is no private right of action. Montana was a confirmed coverage gap — this is the state's comprehensive privacy statute and the wiki had only one Montana page before it.
