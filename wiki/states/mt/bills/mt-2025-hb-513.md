## Summary

Montana HB 513 (2025) revises the state's law on the use of an individual's name, voice, and visual likeness, establishing a property right in them and setting penalties for unauthorized use. It defines a "digital visual depiction" as a replica, imitation, or approximation of an individual's likeness that is nearly indistinguishable from an actual depiction of that individual — the digital-replica concept these statutes use to reach AI-generated likenesses. Remedies include statutory damages or actual damages, whichever is greater, plus any profits from the unauthorized use. ⚠ The latest text LegiScan carries for this bill is the AMENDED version rather than an enrolled copy, so the enacted wording should be confirmed against the chaptered act.
