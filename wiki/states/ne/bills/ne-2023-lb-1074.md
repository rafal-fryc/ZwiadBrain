## Summary

Nebraska's LB 1074 (approved April 17, 2024) is a banking and finance omnibus that also adopts the Data Privacy Act. The Act applies to persons that do business in Nebraska or produce products or services consumed by residents, process or sell personal data, and are not small businesses under the federal Small Business Act, though the ban on selling sensitive data without consumer consent also reaches small businesses. State agencies, political subdivisions, financial institutions, HIPAA covered entities, nonprofits, higher-education institutions, and certain utilities are exempt. Consumers may access, correct, delete, and obtain a portable copy of their data and opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and profiling for significant decisions; controllers must answer within 45 days, offer an appeal, post privacy notices, obtain consent for sensitive data, and conduct data protection assessments. The Attorney General has exclusive enforcement after a 30-day cure period, with civil penalties up to $7,500 per violation and no private right of action. The bill also adopts the Public Entities Pooled Investment Act and changes breach-of-security and other provisions.
