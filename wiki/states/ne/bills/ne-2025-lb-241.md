## Summary

Nebraska LB 241 creates a liability shield for private entities (corporations, associations, partnerships, and similar for-profit or nonprofit business entities) hit by a cybersecurity event affecting nonpublic personal information, such as Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, financial account numbers, or biometric records. A private entity cannot be held liable in a class action arising from a cybersecurity event unless the event was caused by the entity's willful, wanton, or gross negligence. Enacted and approved by the Governor on March 17, 2025.
