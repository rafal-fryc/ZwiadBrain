## Summary

New Hampshire's HB 1186, the Firearm Purchaser's Privacy Act, creates RSA chapter 159-F, barring a payment card acquirer from assigning a firearms-specific merchant category code to a merchant and barring a payment card network from requiring or permitting a merchant to use one; it likewise bars a firearms retailer from providing such a code, limiting retailers to a general-merchandise or sporting-goods merchant category code instead. The law binds payment card acquirers, issuers, networks, and firearms retailers engaged in the lawful sale of firearms, ammunition, or firearms accessories in the state. The Attorney General has exclusive enforcement authority, including subpoena power, a 30-day notice-and-cure opportunity before an action may be brought, and authority to seek injunctions, civil penalties, and attorney's fees for intentional violations. The act took effect January 1, 2025.
