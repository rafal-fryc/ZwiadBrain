## Summary

New Hampshire's SB 255, the New Hampshire Data Privacy Act (RSA chapter 507-H), took effect January 1, 2025 and follows the Virginia/Connecticut comprehensive privacy model. It applies to businesses conducting business in or targeting New Hampshire residents that annually control or process personal data of 35,000 or more unique consumers, or of 10,000 or more consumers while deriving over 25% of gross revenue from selling personal data, subject to standard GLBA/HIPAA/nonprofit/higher-education exemptions. It gives consumers rights to confirm, access, correct, delete, and port their data and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale of personal data, and profiling for solely automated decisions with legal or significant effects, while requiring controllers to minimize data collection, obtain opt-in consent for sensitive data, post a privacy notice, honor universal opt-out signals, and conduct data protection assessments for higher-risk processing. Enforcement rests exclusively with the Attorney General, with a 60-day cure period that sunsets December 31, 2025, and there is no private right of action.
