## Summary

New Jersey's A 4723 requires a motor vehicle dealer that takes possession of a vehicle from a consumer for resale or lease to offer to delete the consumer's personal information stored in the vehicle's systems — such as navigation history, paired phone data, and garage door codes — using NIST-based data-clearing or factory-reset methods. Dealers may charge a reasonable, disclosed fee for the service, and violations carry a civil penalty of $500 for a first offense and $1,000 for subsequent offenses, enforced by the Division of Consumer Affairs. The act took effect immediately upon signing.
