## Summary

New Jersey's S 3125 (Chapter 113) expands "Daniel's Law" to add child protective investigators in the Division of Child Protection and Permanency to the class of protected public employees whose home address and certain other personal information may not be publicly disclosed. Like the law's existing protections for judges, prosecutors, and law enforcement officers, the removal/nondisclosure right is enforceable against government record custodians as well as private persons and data brokers who publish such information online.
