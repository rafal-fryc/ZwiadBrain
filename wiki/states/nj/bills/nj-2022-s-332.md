## Summary

New Jersey's S 332 (P.L. 2023, c. 266, signed January 16, 2024) enacts the New Jersey Data Privacy Act, requiring businesses that control or process the personal data of New Jersey consumers to give them rights to access, correct, delete, and port their data and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale of personal data, and significant-effect profiling. Controllers must limit data collection to what is reasonably necessary, obtain consent before processing sensitive data (including that of a known child), and provide clear privacy notices and appeal processes for denied requests, with the Attorney General as enforcer. The Act takes effect January 15, 2025.
