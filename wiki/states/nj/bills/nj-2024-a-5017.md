## Summary

Enacted as P.L.2025, c.367 (approved January 20, 2026), this law amends New Jersey's consumer personal-data notification/disclosure statute, the New Jersey Data Privacy Act (P.L.2023, c.266, codified at C.56:8-166.4 et seq.), by revising the exemptions in Section 10 (C.56:8-166.13) and updating a definition in Section 1 (C.56:8-166.4). The exemption list, which already excused HIPAA-covered protected health information, Gramm-Leach-Bliley financial institutions, insurance institutions under P.L.1985 c.179, the Motor Vehicle Commission's DPPA-permitted data sales, FCRA-governed consumer reporting agencies, state/local government bodies, and certain human-subjects research, is expanded to also cover information treated like protected health information handled by HIPAA-covered entities or business associates, additional human-subjects research conducted under ICH good clinical practice guidelines or 21 C.F.R. Parts 50 and 56, insurance-support organizations as defined in C.17:23A-2, and national securities associations registered under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934. It affects controllers and processors otherwise subject to the Data Privacy Act's notification and disclosure requirements, and took effect immediately upon approval.
