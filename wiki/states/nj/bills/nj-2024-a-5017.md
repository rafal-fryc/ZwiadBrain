## Summary

Enacted as P.L.2025, c.367 (approved January 20, 2026), this law amends New Jersey's consumer personal-data notification/disclosure statute, the New Jersey Data Privacy Act (P.L.2023, c.266, codified at C.56:8-166.4 et seq.), by revising the exemptions in Section 10 (C.56:8-166.13) and updating a definition in Section 1 (C.56:8-166.4). The exemption list, which already excused HIPAA-covered protected health information, Gramm-Leach-Bliley financial institutions, insurance institutions under P.L.1985 c.179, the Motor Vehicle Commission's DPPA-permitted data sales, FCRA-governed consumer reporting agencies, state/local government bodies, and certain human-subjects research, is expanded to also cover information treated like protected health information handled by HIPAA-covered entities or business associates, additional human-subjects research conducted under ICH good clinical practice guidelines or 21 C.F.R. Parts 50 and 56, insurance-support organizations as defined in C.17:23A-2, and national securities associations registered under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934. It affects controllers and processors otherwise subject to the Data Privacy Act's notification and disclosure requirements, and took effect immediately upon approval.

## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

Assemblywoman Ellen Park introduced A5017 on November 14, 2024, with co-sponsors including Asm. Cody Miller, Sen. Raj Mukherji, and Asw. Garnet Hall, to expand the categories of personal information and entities exempted from the notification and disclosure requirements of the New Jersey Data Privacy Act (P.L.2023, c.266; C.56:8-166.4 et seq.). The bill was first referred to the Assembly Science, Innovation and Technology Committee, which reported it favorably with amendments on a 7-0 vote on January 23, 2025, revising the bill so that insurance-support organizations, as a whole (rather than just their personal data), would be exempt from the Act's requirements. The bill then moved to the Assembly Financial Institutions and Insurance Committee, which on May 15, 2025 reported the First Reprint favorably with further amendments adding an exemption for national securities associations registered under Section 15A of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934. The full Assembly passed the bill 77-0-0 on May 22, 2025, after which it was received in the Senate and referred to the Senate Commerce Committee. That committee reported the Second Reprint favorably with amendments on November 17, 2025 — broadening the exemptions to cover information handled like protected health information by HIPAA covered entities and business associates, human subjects research conducted under International Council for Harmonisation good clinical practice guidelines, and an expanded definition of de-identified data — and noted that A5017 (2R), as amended, was identical to companion bill S4259. On December 18, 2025, the Senate substituted A5017 for S4259 and passed the bill 38-0; the Assembly received it the same day for concurrence, and on January 12, 2026 the Assembly passed the bill on concurrence 75-0-0, completing passage of both houses. Outgoing Governor Phil Murphy approved the measure on his final day in office, January 20, 2026, enacting it as P.L.2025, c.367, which amends the definitions in Section 1 (C.56:8-166.4) and the exemptions in Section 10 (C.56:8-166.13) of the New Jersey Data Privacy Act to add insurance-support organizations, national securities associations, HIPAA-equivalent health information, and expanded human-subjects-research and de-identified-data provisions to the law's existing exemptions, and took effect immediately upon approval.
