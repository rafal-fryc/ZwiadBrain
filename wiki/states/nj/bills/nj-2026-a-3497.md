## Summary

New Jersey A3497, the Forbidding the Algorithmic Inflation of Rent (FAIR) Act, makes it a violation of the state Antitrust Act for a rental property owner to pay for or use an algorithmic 'coordinator' service that pools competitively sensitive lease data (prices, terms, occupancy) from multiple landlords to set or recommend rents; for a coordinator to facilitate such price-setting; and for landlords to engage in parallel algorithmic pricing coordination more broadly. The Attorney General must establish a complaint intake mechanism and may adopt implementing rules. Takes effect on the first day of the twelfth month after enactment.
