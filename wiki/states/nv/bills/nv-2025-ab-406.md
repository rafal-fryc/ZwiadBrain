## Summary

Nevada AB 406 (2025) is a health omnibus with several distinct AI provisions. Its public-school provisions — prohibiting certain uses of AI in public schools and directing the Department of Education to develop an AI policy — govern the state's own operations. The bill is tracked for its private-facing provisions: it imposes restrictions on the marketing and programming of artificial intelligence systems, prohibits certain persons from representing themselves as qualified to provide mental or behavioral health care, restricts providers of mental or behavioral health care in their use of AI, and attaches civil penalties. Those duties fall on AI system providers and health care providers rather than on the state.
