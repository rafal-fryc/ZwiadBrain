## Summary

New York's SHIELD Act (Chapter 117 of the Laws of 2019) expands the state's data-breach notification law by broadening the definition of 'private information' to include biometric data and account, credit, and debit card numbers, and widening what qualifies as a reportable breach. It also imposes reasonable data-security safeguard requirements on any business that owns or licenses private information of New York residents, with increased civil penalties for violations.
