## Summary

Enacted via approval memo 58 (December 2022), this law establishes security and responsibility requirements for privately operated critical energy infrastructure in New York, covering generation, transmission, and storage facilities. Operators of covered energy infrastructure must meet defined security standards aimed at protecting those systems from physical and cyber threats. The law also sets a broader framework for critical infrastructure protection across the state.
