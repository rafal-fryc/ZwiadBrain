## Summary

Signed as Chapter 417 of 2023, this law extends New York's critical infrastructure security and cybersecurity obligations beyond energy systems to a broader range of privately operated facilities, including hazardous-substance storage sites, commercial aviation infrastructure, and petroleum and natural gas fuel transmission pipelines. Covered operators must meet security and cybersecurity requirements designed to protect those facilities from physical and cyber threats. The law fills gaps left by earlier energy-focused statutes by bringing additional high-risk infrastructure categories under the same regulatory framework.

## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

Assembly Bill A7364 was introduced on May 18, 2023 by Assemblymember Steven Otis, with co-sponsors Angelo Santabarbara and Jacob (Jake Ryan) Blumencranz, and referred to the Assembly Committee on Science and Technology; a same-as companion, Senate Bill S7414, was introduced May 24, 2023 by Senator Kristen Gonzalez and referred to the Senate Committee on Internet and Technology. The Assembly bill was reported and advanced through Rules, passed the Assembly on May 31, 2023, and was delivered to the Senate, where on June 6, 2023 it was substituted for S7414, discharged from committee, placed on the Third Reading Calendar, and passed the Senate 62-0 with one member excused, before being returned to the Assembly. The legislation amends sections 713, 714, and 716 of the Executive Law to clarify that the Division of Homeland Security and Emergency Services' review and analysis responsibilities for critical infrastructure -- energy generating and transmission facilities, storage facilities for hazardous substances, and commercial aviation, petroleum and natural gas fuel transmission facilities and pipelines -- expressly include cyber security, building on the cyber-defense framework enacted by Chapter 743 of the Laws of 2022. The bill was delivered to Governor Kathy Hochul on September 7, 2023, and she signed it into law on September 15, 2023 as Chapter 417 of the Laws of 2023.
