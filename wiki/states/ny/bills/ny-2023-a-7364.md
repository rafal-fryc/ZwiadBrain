## Summary

Signed as Chapter 417 of 2023, this law extends New York's critical infrastructure security and cybersecurity obligations beyond energy systems to a broader range of privately operated facilities, including hazardous-substance storage sites, commercial aviation infrastructure, and petroleum and natural gas fuel transmission pipelines. Covered operators must meet security and cybersecurity requirements designed to protect those facilities from physical and cyber threats. The law fills gaps left by earlier energy-focused statutes by bringing additional high-risk infrastructure categories under the same regulatory framework.
