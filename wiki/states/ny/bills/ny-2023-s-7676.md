## Summary

NY S 7676 establishes that contract provisions allowing the use of a digital replica of a performer's voice or likeness to replace work the performer would otherwise have performed are unenforceable unless the provision contains a reasonably specific description of the intended uses and the performer was represented by counsel or a union. The law mirrors California AB 2602 in its substantive requirements and is directed at preventing overbroad AI-substitution clauses in performer contracts.
