  narrative: The Stop Addictive Feeds Exploitation (SAFE) for Kids Act began as Senate
    bill S7694, introduced on October 13, 2023, by Senator Andrew Gounardes as lead
    Senate sponsor, with Assembly companion A8148 introduced by Assemblymember Nily
    Rozic. The bill was referred first to the Rules Committee and then to the Senate
    Internet and Technology Committee under chair Senator Kevin Parker, which coordinated
    extended stakeholder negotiations involving the offices of Attorney General Letitia
    James and Governor Kathy Hochul. After substantive revisions — including refined
    age-verification requirements and expanded parental-consent standards — both chambers
    amended the bills to their final forms (S7694A and A8148A) in early June 2024.
    On June 6, 2024, the Senate Rules Committee voted 20-0 (with four members recording
    reservations) to advance S7694A to the floor, and the full Senate passed it unanimously
    by a vote of 61-0, making New York the first state legislature in the nation to
    pass restrictions on algorithmic addictive feeds for minors. The Assembly followed
    on June 7, 2024, substituting A8148A with the Senate-passed S7694A and passing
    it. Governor Hochul signed S7694A and the companion Child Data Protection Act
    (S7695B) into law as Chapter 120 of the Laws of 2024 on June 20, 2024; the SAFE
    for Kids Act takes effect 180 days after the Attorney General promulgates implementing
    regulations.

## Summary

Establishes the Stop Addictive Feeds Exploitation (SAFE) For Kids Act prohibiting the provision of addictive feeds to minors by addictive social media platforms; establishes remedies and penalties.

## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

The Stop Addictive Feeds Exploitation (SAFE) for Kids Act began as Senate bill S7694, introduced on October 13, 2023, by Senator Andrew Gounardes as lead Senate sponsor, with Assembly companion A8148 introduced by Assemblymember Nily Rozic. The bill was referred first to the Rules Committee and then to the Senate Internet and Technology Committee under chair Senator Kevin Parker, which coordinated extended stakeholder negotiations involving the offices of Attorney General Letitia James and Governor Kathy Hochul. After substantive revisions — including refined age-verification requirements and expanded parental-consent standards — both chambers amended the bills to their final forms (S7694A and A8148A) in early June 2024. On June 6, 2024, the Senate Rules Committee voted 20-0 (with four members recording reservations) to advance S7694A to the floor, and the full Senate passed it unanimously by a vote of 61-0, making New York the first state legislature in the nation to pass restrictions on algorithmic addictive feeds for minors. The Assembly followed on June 7, 2024, substituting A8148A with the Senate-passed S7694A and passing it. Governor Hochul signed S7694A and the companion Child Data Protection Act (S7695B) into law as Chapter 120 of the Laws of 2024 on June 20, 2024; the SAFE for Kids Act takes effect 180 days after the Attorney General promulgates implementing regulations.
