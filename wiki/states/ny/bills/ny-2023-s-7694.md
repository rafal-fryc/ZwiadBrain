## Summary

The Stop Addictive Feeds Exploitation (SAFE) For Kids Act prohibits operators of social media platforms with algorithmically personalized feeds from providing those addictive feeds to users under 18 without verified parental consent. The law also restricts covered platforms from sending nighttime notifications to minor users. Enforcement is by the New York Attorney General with civil penalties available for violations.
