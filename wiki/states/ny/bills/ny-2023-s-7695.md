## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

The New York Child Data Protection Act (S 7695) was introduced in the New York Senate on October 13, 2023 by Senator Andrew Gounardes and initially referred to the Rules Committee before being recommitted to the Committee on Internet and Technology on January 3, 2024. The bill was amended twice — as S7695A on March 12, 2024 and as S7695B on June 3, 2024 — refining its consent framework and scope before reaching the floor. On June 6, 2024, the Senate passed S7695B by a vote of 61 to 0, the same day the companion SAFE for Kids Act (S7694) also cleared the chamber. The Assembly companion bill (A8149A), sponsored by Assemblymember Nily Rozic and referred to the Assembly Committee on Science and Technology, passed the Assembly on June 7, 2024 by a vote of 144 to 0, with S7695B substituted for A8149A at that stage. Governor Kathy Hochul signed both bills into law on June 20, 2024 (Chapter 121), making New York the first state to enact comprehensive legislation restricting digital services from collecting or using the personal data of minors under 18 without informed consent and requiring contractual safeguards on third-party data sharing. The law took effect June 23, 2025, with enforcement authority vested in the Attorney General.
