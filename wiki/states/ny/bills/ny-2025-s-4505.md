## Summary

New York's S4505, signed as Chapter 689 of 2025, adds Article 45-A to the general business law and section 7.48 to the mental hygiene law to require warning labels on "addictive social media platforms" — services whose primary function is media-sharing among users and that offer an addictive feed, autoplay, infinite scroll, like counts, and/or push notifications. Covered operators must display a warning label (text and manner set by the Commissioner of Mental Health, in consultation with the Commissioners of Health and Education) each time a covered user accesses the platform, and are barred from burying the label in terms of service, obscuring it, mismatching its timing/duration, or degrading service to punish its display. The attorney general may promulgate implementing rules and enforce the law through civil actions seeking injunctions, restitution, disgorgement, damages, and penalties up to $5,000 per violation; the act took effect 180 days after the AG adopted implementing regulations.
