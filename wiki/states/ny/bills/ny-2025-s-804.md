## Summary

New York's S804, signed as Chapter 91 of 2025, amends General Business Law §899-aa(8)(a) to change how data breach notification is provided to the Department of Financial Services (DFS). Previously all entities notifying the state of a breach affecting New York residents had to separately notify DFS as part of the multi-agency notice; as amended, notice to DFS is required only for "covered entities" as defined in DFS cybersecurity regulation 23 NYCRR 500.1, and that notice must be made in compliance with 23 NYCRR 500.17 (DFS's existing cybersecurity incident-notification rule) rather than under the general business law's separate notice process. The amendment narrows and aligns breach-notification obligations to DFS specifically for financial institutions already regulated under DFS's cybersecurity rules, while notice to the attorney general, department of state, and state police remains otherwise unchanged.
