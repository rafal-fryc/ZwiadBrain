        Introduced  by  Sen.  COMRIE -- read twice and ordered printed, and when
          printed to be committed to the Committee on Rules
 
        AN ACT to amend the general business law, in relation to  when  and  how
          notification  of  a data breach is to be provided to the department of
          financial services` **`The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and  Assem- bly, do enact as follows:`** `1    Section  1.  Paragraph  (a)  of subdivision 8 of section 899-aa of the
     2  general business law, as amended by a chapter of the laws of 2024 amend-
     3  ing the general business law relating to notification of a data  breach,
     4  as  proposed  in  legislative bills numbers S. 2659-B and A.  8872-A, is
     5  amended to read as follows:
     6    (a) In the event that any New York residents are to be  notified,  the
     7  person  or business shall notify the state attorney general, the depart-
     8  ment of state, the division of  state  police,  and  the  department  of
     9  financial  services  as  to  the timing, content and distribution of the
    10  notices and approximate number of affected persons and shall  provide  a
    11  copy  of  the template of the notice sent to affected persons` **`; provided,`** `12` **`however, that notice to the department of financial services shall  only`** `13` **`be required if the person or business is a covered entity, as defined in`** `14` **`23  NYCRR 500.1, and provided further that such notice shall be provided`** `15` **`to the department of financial services  in  compliance  with  23  NYCRR`** `16` **`500.17`** `.  Such  notice  shall be made without delaying notice to affected
    17  New York residents.
    18    § 2. This act shall take effect on the  same  date  and  in  the  same
    19  manner  as  a  chapter of the laws of 2024 amending the general business
    20  law relating to notification of a data breach, as proposed  in  legisla-
    21  tive bills numbers S. 2659-B and A. 8872-A, takes effect.
