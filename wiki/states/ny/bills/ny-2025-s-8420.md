## Summary

New York S8420-A amends General Business Law §396-b to require that any commercial advertisement using a 'synthetic performer' — an AI- or software-generated digital asset made to look like an audiovisual performance by an unidentifiable human — conspicuously disclose that use where the advertiser has actual knowledge of it. Violations carry a civil penalty of $1,000 for a first offense and $5,000 for subsequent offenses. Expressive works (film, television, games and the like), audio-only advertisements, and AI used solely for language translation are exempt; existing rights under the state's name and likeness statute and Section 230 are preserved. Chapter 617; effective 180 days after signing.
