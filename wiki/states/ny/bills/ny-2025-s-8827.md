## Summary

New York's S8827, signed as Chapter 85 of 2025, is the chapter amendment to S4505's addictive-platform warning-label law, enacted in the same 2025-2026 session before the original law's regulations took full effect. It broadens the regulated category from "addictive social media platforms" to "addictive feature platforms" (defined by addictive feed, autoplay, and/or infinite scroll, dropping like counts and push notifications as triggering features), replaces the AG-designed warning text with a fixed statutory Surgeon General warning, sets specific display triggers and durations (label shown on initial daily access for 10+ seconds occupying 25%+ of the screen, then again after three hours of cumulative use and hourly thereafter for 30+ seconds occupying 75%+ of the screen), exempts users the operator reasonably believes are over 17, and repeals and re-enacts mental hygiene law §7.48 by eliminating the Commissioner of Mental Health's role in designing/updating the label text. The amended law's effective date is set at January 1, 2027.
