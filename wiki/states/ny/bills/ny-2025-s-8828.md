## Summary

New York S8828, the RAISE Act (Responsible AI Safety and Education Act), requires large frontier developers — companies that, with affiliates, had over $500 million in prior-year revenue and have trained AI models above a specified computing-power threshold — to publish and maintain a frontier AI safety framework covering catastrophic-risk thresholds, mitigations, cybersecurity for model weights, and incident response, and to publish transparency reports before deploying new or materially modified frontier models. Developers must report critical safety incidents to a new oversight office in the Department of Financial Services within 72 hours, or 24 hours where there is imminent risk of death or injury, and file periodic disclosure statements. The Attorney General may seek civil penalties up to $1 million for a first violation and $3 million for subsequent violations; no private right of action. Replaces an earlier 2025 frontier-AI law and takes effect January 1, 2027.
