## Summary

Oklahoma SB 546 enacts a comprehensive consumer data privacy law establishing consumer rights, an appeal process for denied requests, privacy-notice requirements, data protection assessment obligations, and penalty and liability provisions. ⚠ Text extraction produced only an 'Enrolled' status marker, so this summary is limited to the act's stated subject matter and needs verification against the enacted text.
