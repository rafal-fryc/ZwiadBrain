## Summary

Oklahoma's SB 546, approved by the Governor on March 20, 2026, enacts a comprehensive consumer data privacy law codified in Title 75A. Consumers may confirm and access, correct, delete, and obtain a portable copy of their personal data and opt out of targeted advertising, sale of personal data, and profiling for decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Controllers must respond within 45 days (extendable once by 45), may charge only for manifestly unfounded, excessive, or repetitive requests after two free responses a year, and must run an appeal process. The act also covers privacy notices, processor contracts, data protection assessments, Attorney General enforcement with a cure period and penalties, and a rule that sensitive data (including a known child's data under 13 and precise geolocation within 1,750 feet) is processed only with consent.
