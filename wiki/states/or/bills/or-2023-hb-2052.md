## Summary

Oregon's HB 2052, enacted in 2023, requires "data brokers" — businesses that collect and sell or license Oregon residents' brokered personal data (name, address, biometric data, Social Security number, etc.) to third parties — to register annually with the Department of Consumer and Business Services and disclose consumers' opt-out options. Registration exempts entities already covered by federal credit-reporting or financial-privacy law and several publisher/directory activities; violations carry civil penalties up to $500 per violation ($10,000 annual cap). The section became operative January 1, 2024, and the act carried an emergency clause taking effect on passage (signed June 2023).
