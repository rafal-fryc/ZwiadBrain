## Summary

Oregon's SB 619 (2023) establishes the Oregon Consumer Privacy Act, requiring businesses ("controllers") that process personal data of Oregon residents to give consumers rights to access, correct, delete, and obtain a portable copy of their data, and to opt out of targeted advertising, data sales, and profiling. Sensitive data (such as health, biometric, or precise geolocation information) may only be processed with the consumer's opt-in consent. The law takes effect July 1, 2024, with staggered compliance for nonprofits.
