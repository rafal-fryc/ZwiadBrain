## Summary

Oregon HB 2008 amends the Oregon Consumer Privacy Act (ORS 646A.578) to bar a controller from selling personal data that accurately identifies a consumer's or their device's present or past location within a 1,750-foot radius, exempting communications content and utility-metering data, and to bar selling personal data of, or targeting advertising or legal-effect profiling toward, a consumer the controller knows or willfully disregards is under 16 (raised from the prior 13-15 age band). It also raises the child-consent tie to the federal COPPA standard for processing sensitive data of known children and requires controllers to honor consumer opt-out preference signals under specified technical criteria. The bill was passed by the Oregon House and Senate in 2025 and signed by Governor Tina Kotek, binding controllers subject to the Oregon Consumer Privacy Act.
