## Summary

Oregon HB 3875 amends ORS 646A.572 to extend the Oregon Consumer Privacy Act to any motor vehicle manufacturer and its affiliates that control or process personal data obtained from a consumer's use of a motor vehicle or any vehicle component, regardless of whether the manufacturer meets the Act's usual 100,000-consumer or 25,000-consumer/25%-revenue processing thresholds. Manufacturers and affiliates covered this way remain subject to the Act's existing exemptions (e.g., HIPAA-covered health data, FCRA-governed credit data, GLBA-covered financial data, insurance entities, employment records) and its general carve-outs for legal compliance, fraud/security response, contract performance, and similar activities. This subjects vehicle telematics/connected-car data controllers to the OCPA's consumer notice, consent, and opt-out framework; the act takes effect on the 91st day after the 2025 regular legislative session adjourns sine die, and was passed by both chambers and signed by Governor Tina Kotek.
