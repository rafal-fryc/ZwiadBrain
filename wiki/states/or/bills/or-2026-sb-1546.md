## Summary

Oregon SB 1546 requires operators of artificial intelligence companion platforms to disclose that the user is interacting with AI whenever a reasonable person could otherwise believe they are talking to a human, and to maintain an evidence-based protocol referring users who express suicidal or self-harm ideation to the 988 crisis lifeline (or a youth line for users under 25), publishing both the protocol and annual referral statistics. For known minor users, operators must prevent sentience or humanizing claims, romantic or sexual simulation, adult-minor role-play, variable engagement rewards, and guilt-inducing messages discouraging disengagement, and must give periodic reminders that the companion is AI. The act creates a private right of action for actual damages or $1,000 statutory damages per violation, injunctive relief, and attorney's fees.
