## Summary

Pennsylvania's HB 739, enacted in 2024 as the Insurance Data Security Act, adds a new chapter to Title 40 adopting the NAIC Insurance Data Security Model Law. It requires insurers and other insurance licensees regulated by the Pennsylvania Insurance Department to develop, implement, and maintain a written information security program based on a risk assessment, investigate cybersecurity events affecting nonpublic information, and notify the Insurance Commissioner when such events occur; smaller licensees falling below specified employee/revenue thresholds are exempt. The Insurance Department enforces the law and may impose penalties for noncompliance.
