## Summary

Rhode Island's H 7281 (Substitute A) adds an insurer cybersecurity framework to the insurance code. Each domestic insurer must develop, implement, and maintain a written information security program based on a risk assessment, with board oversight, third-party service provider diligence, a written incident response plan, prompt investigation of cybersecurity events, five-year record retention, and an annual compliance certification to the commissioner by April 15. A domestic insurer must notify the commissioner as promptly as possible, and no later than three business days after determining that a cybersecurity event has occurred, if notice is required to another government body or the event is reasonably likely to materially harm a Rhode Island consumer or a material part of the insurer's operations. It also makes a minor wording change to the director's insurer examination authority.
