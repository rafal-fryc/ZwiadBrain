## Summary

Rhode Island's H 7281 (2024) amends the state's insurer examination laws to add cybersecurity-event provisions, giving state insurance regulators authority to examine and investigate cybersecurity events affecting licensed insurers as part of the state's NAIC-model-based insurer data security oversight. An identical companion bill, S 2802, was also enacted.
