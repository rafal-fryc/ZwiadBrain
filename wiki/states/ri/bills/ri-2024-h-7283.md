## Summary

Rhode Island's H 7283 (2024), primarily an insurance producer licensing housekeeping bill amending definitions, fees, and termination-notice rules under the Producer Licensing Act and Claims Adjusters chapter, also adds an Insurance Data Security Act component requiring licensed insurance entities to maintain a written information security program and to investigate and provide notice of qualifying cybersecurity events. An identical companion bill, S 2798, was also enacted.
