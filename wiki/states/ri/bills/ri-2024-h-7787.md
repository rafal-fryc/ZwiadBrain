## Summary

Rhode Island's H 7787 (2024) enacts the Rhode Island Data Transparency and Privacy Protection Act, a comprehensive consumer privacy law giving residents rights to access, correct, delete, and port their personal data and to opt out of targeted advertising, data sales, and significant-effect profiling, while imposing data minimization, security, and assessment duties on covered controllers and processors. An identical companion bill, S 2500, was also enacted; the law takes effect January 1, 2026.
