## Summary

Rhode Island's H 7787 (Substitute A as amended) creates the Rhode Island Data Transparency and Privacy Protection Act (R.I. Gen. Laws chapter 6-48.1). Commercial websites and internet service providers doing business in Rhode Island must designate a controller and disclose the categories of personal data collected, the third parties to whom customer data is or may be sold, and a contact address, and must clearly disclose any sale or targeted advertising. The substantive duties and rights apply to for-profit entities that, in the preceding year, processed personal data of at least 35,000 customers (excluding payment-transaction data), or at least 10,000 customers while deriving more than 20 percent of gross revenue from selling personal data. Customers may confirm and access, correct, delete, and port their data and opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and solely automated profiling with significant effects; controllers must respond within 45 days, offer an appeal decided within 60 days, obtain consent for sensitive data, and assess high-risk processing. Government bodies, nonprofits, higher-education institutions, GLBA financial institutions, and HIPAA covered entities are exempt.

## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

H 7787, the Rhode Island Data Transparency and Privacy Protection Act, was introduced by Rep. Evan Shanley and co-sponsors Reps. Batista, Donovan, Edwards, Solomon, and Voas and referred to committee on 2024-02-29. The committee initially recommended the bill be held for further study on 2024-03-28, a common procedural step for complex measures, before returning with a recommendation to pass a Substitute A version on 2024-06-06. The House passed Sub A as amended on 2024-06-10 by a vote of 71-1, and the Senate passed the same Substitute A in concurrence on 2024-06-13. An identical companion measure, S 2500, moved in parallel and was likewise enacted. The bill was transmitted to Governor Dan McKee on 2024-06-21 and became law without his signature on 2024-06-29. As enacted, the RIDTPPA establishes controller and processor duties for entities handling Rhode Islanders' personal data, grants consumers rights to access, correct, delete, and obtain a portable copy of their data, and gives consumers the right to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and profiling in furtherance of decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects. The Act is enforced exclusively by the Rhode Island Attorney General and takes effect 2026-01-01.
