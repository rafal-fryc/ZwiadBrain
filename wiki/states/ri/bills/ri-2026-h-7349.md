## Summary

Rhode Island H 7349 enacts the Oversight of Artificial Intelligence Technology in Mental Health Care Act, adding Chapter 40.1-5.5 to the general laws. It bars licensed therapy or psychotherapy providers from using AI designed to simulate emotional attachment/bonding or AI companions for mental-health/emotional support in recorded or transcribed sessions unless the patient (or guardian) is informed in writing that AI will be used, told its specific purpose, and gives consent, and it separately bars any individual or entity from offering therapy or psychotherapy services to the public via internet-based AI unless delivered by a state-licensed professional. Even with consent, providers may not let AI make independent therapeutic decisions, conduct unsupervised therapeutic communication, or determine treatment plans/recommendations, though administrative and supplementary support (e.g., scheduling, note-taking, data trends subject to review) remain permitted, with FDA-cleared tools and IRB-approved research exempted. The executive office of health and human services may investigate violations and impose the confidentiality penalties under section 5-37.3-9; the act took effect upon passage.
