## Summary

Rhode Island H 7538 enacts the Use of Artificial Intelligence by Healthcare Providers Notification Act (title 23, chapter 106), requiring any healthcare provider (physicians, PAs, dentists, nurses, and other state-licensed health professionals) or healthcare facility that uses AI to document in-person or telehealth visits to notify the patient of that AI use and to review the AI-generated documentation for accuracy after the visit. The disclosure duty is triggered specifically by AI used to memorialize/document the encounter (e.g., AI scribe tools), not AI use generally. The act took effect upon passage.
