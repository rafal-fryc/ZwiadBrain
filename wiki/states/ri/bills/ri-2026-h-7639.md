## Summary

Rhode Island H 7639, the House companion to S 2203, establishes a data-privacy regime for direct-to-consumer genetic testing companies operating in the state, requiring them to give consumers clear information about how their genetic data is collected, used, and shared. It is expected to require express consumer consent before genetic data is collected or disclosed, along with consumer rights to access and delete their genetic data and obligations on companies to maintain data security and transparency about data practices. The bill is part of a broader 2026 multi-state wave of direct-to-consumer genetic-privacy legislation alongside measures in Alabama, Tennessee, and Vermont; specific statutory section numbers and dates are not confirmed here.
