## Summary

South Carolina H4591, the Stop Harm from Addictive Social Media Act, applies to covered social media platforms generating at least $1 billion in worldwide annual advertising revenue. Platforms must estimate account holders' ages — to 80% and then 90% confidence after set usage thresholds — to identify South Carolina residents under 16 as children; obtain verifiable parental consent before creating or continuing a child's account; default child accounts to the most private settings; and bar addictive interface features (infinite scroll, autoplay, push notifications, engagement metrics and the like) and profile-based advertising or feeds for children. Contracts formed with unconsented child accounts are void. The act creates a private right of action for children and parents, with a $10,000 statutory-damages minimum for reckless or knowing violations and punitive damages for a pattern of violation, alongside Attorney General enforcement. Effective January 1, 2027.
