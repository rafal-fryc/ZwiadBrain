## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

HB 1181, the Tennessee Information Protection Act, was filed by House Majority Whip Johnny Garrett and referred to the House Banking & Consumer Affairs Subcommittee on 2023-02-07 before moving through the House Commerce Committee and Calendar & Rules Committee. The House passed the bill as amended on 2023-04-10 by a vote of 90-0. The bill was received in the Senate on 2023-04-12, where, on 2023-04-21, the Senate substituted HB 1181 for its identical companion, SB 73, carried by Sen. Bo Watson, and passed it 29-0 — reflecting the bill's crossfiled, bipartisan-backed path through both chambers rather than any substantive contest on the floor. The House Speaker signed the bill on 2023-05-02 and the Senate Speaker on 2023-05-03, and it was transmitted to Governor Bill Lee on 2023-05-05. Governor Lee signed the measure into law on 2023-05-11, and it was published as Public Chapter 408 on 2023-05-24. The enacted law follows the Virginia-model framework for comprehensive consumer privacy: it creates consumer rights to access, correct, delete, and port personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal information, and profiling, while giving controllers and processors a distinctive affirmative defense against liability if they maintain a written privacy program that reasonably conforms to the NIST Privacy Framework. Enforcement is reserved exclusively to the Tennessee Attorney General, with no private right of action, and the Act takes effect 2025-07-01.
