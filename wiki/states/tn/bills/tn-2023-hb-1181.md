## Summary

Tennessee's HB 1181, the Tennessee Information Protection Act (TIPA), is a comprehensive consumer data privacy law effective July 1, 2025. It applies to entities doing business in or targeting Tennessee residents that exceed $25 million in annual revenue and either control/process the personal information of at least 175,000 consumers, or at least 25,000 consumers while deriving more than half of gross revenue from selling personal information. It gives consumers rights to access, correct, delete, and port their personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale of data, and profiling, while requiring controllers to issue privacy notices, minimize data collection, and conduct assessments for higher-risk processing. Notably, it offers controllers/processors an affirmative defense if they maintain a written privacy program conforming to the NIST Privacy Framework, and it is enforced solely by the Tennessee Attorney General with no private right of action.
