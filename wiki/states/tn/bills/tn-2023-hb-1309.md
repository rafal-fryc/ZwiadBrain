## Summary

Tennessee's HB 1309 (Public Chapter 246) amends the state's genetic information nondiscrimination law (TCA Title 56, Chapter 7, Part 27) to extend its protections specifically to life insurance. As enacted, it bars a life insurance provider from canceling coverage for an individual or family member based solely on genetic information, from requiring a genetic test (including complete genome sequencing) as a precondition of life insurance coverage, and from accessing an individual's sensitive medical or genetic information without the individual's signed written consent; it does not prevent insurers from using existing health information, including genetic information already in a medical record, for underwriting. The act also updates prior statutory references to distinguish 'health insurance provider' from the newly defined 'life insurance provider.' It was passed by the House on March 30, 2023, and applies to life insurance policies entered into, issued, amended, or renewed on or after July 1, 2023.
