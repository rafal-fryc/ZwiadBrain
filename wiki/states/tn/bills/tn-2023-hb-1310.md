## Summary

Tennessee's HB 1310, the Genetic Information Privacy Act, regulates direct-to-consumer genetic testing companies' handling of consumers' genetic data. It requires the companies to obtain express consent before collecting, using, or disclosing genetic data, to maintain clear privacy policies, and to provide reasonable security for that data. Consumers are given the right to access their genetic data and to request deletion of their account along with destruction of their biological sample.
