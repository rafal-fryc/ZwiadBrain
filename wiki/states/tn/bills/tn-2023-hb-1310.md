## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

HB 1310, the Tennessee Genetic Information Privacy Act, was introduced by Rep. Sabi Kumar to amend Tennessee Code Annotated Title 47, requiring direct-to-consumer genetic-testing companies to obtain express consent before collecting, using, or disclosing a consumer's genetic data, to provide consumers access and deletion rights, to maintain reasonable data-security practices, and to be subject to enforcement by the Tennessee Attorney General. The House Banking & Consumer Affairs Subcommittee took up the bill on its final calendar on March 21, 2023, and the House Commerce Committee considered it on March 28, 2023, before it reached the Calendar & Rules Committee on March 30, 2023. The full House passed HB 1310 as amended by a vote of 90-0 on April 6, 2023. The Senate received the House bill on April 10, 2023, substituted it for its own companion measure, and passed it 33-0 that same day. Following engrossment, the bill was signed by the Speakers of both chambers on April 17, 2023, transmitted to Governor Bill Lee on April 18, 2023, and signed into law by the Governor on April 28, 2023, becoming Public Chapter 324. The Act took effect July 1, 2023.
