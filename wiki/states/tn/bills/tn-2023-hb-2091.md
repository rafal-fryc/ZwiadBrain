## Summary

Tennessee's HB 2091, the ELVIS Act (Ensuring Likeness, Voice, and Image Security Act), enacted as Public Chapter 588 effective July 1, 2024, amends the state's Personal Rights Protection Act to add 'voice' as a protected personal right alongside name, photograph, image, and likeness — reaching unauthorized AI-generated voice or likeness simulations. It also creates secondary liability for anyone who distributes, transmits, or makes available an algorithm, software, tool, or other technology or service whose primary purpose is to produce a particular individual's unauthorized voice or likeness. Violations carry civil liability, including injunctive relief and damages, and are separately classified as Class A misdemeanors, with standing extending to affected individuals, their exclusive licensees, and successors.
