## Summary

Tennessee's HB 2434, enacted as Public Chapter 991 and codified at TCA 29-34-215 effective July 1, 2024, creates a safe harbor shielding private entities from class-action liability arising from a cybersecurity event. Under the law, such an entity cannot be held liable in a class action unless the cybersecurity event was caused by its willful and wanton misconduct or gross negligence. Unlike similar laws in some other states, it does not condition this protection on the entity having adopted any specific recognized cybersecurity framework, and it leaves individual (non-class) claims unaffected.
