## Summary

Tennessee SB 1580 (Public Chapter 647) prohibits any person from developing or deploying an artificial intelligence system that advertises or represents to the public that it is, or is able to act as, a qualified mental health professional. A violation is an unfair or deceptive act under the Tennessee Consumer Protection Act of 1977 and carries a specific civil penalty of $5,000 per violation, overriding the Act's ordinary penalty cap. Took effect July 1, 2026.
