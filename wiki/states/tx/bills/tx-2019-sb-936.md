## Summary

Texas SB 936 directs the Public Utility Commission of Texas, in coordination with ERCOT, to establish an independent cybersecurity monitor that oversees and coordinates cybersecurity efforts across electric utilities operating in the state. Participation is mandatory for utilities within the ERCOT footprint and available to certain utilities outside it. The law adds provisions to the Utilities Code imposing cybersecurity-program obligations on private electric-utility operators as critical infrastructure.
