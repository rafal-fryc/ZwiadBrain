## Summary

The Texas Consumer Privacy Act Phase I restricts the disclosure, sale, and use of personal information held in state records — chiefly motor vehicle records under Transportation Code chapter 730 (Texas's Driver's Privacy Protection Act analog) and Parks and Wildlife customer records. It tightens the permitted uses that private authorized recipients of that data (such as resellers and insurers) may make of it, generally requiring express consent for disclosure, and creates criminal offenses (and increases an existing one) for misuse or unauthorized redisclosure of the data.
