## Summary

The Lone Star Infrastructure Protection Act prohibits businesses and governmental entities in Texas from entering contracts that would grant a company owned or controlled by individuals or entities tied to designated hostile nations — China, Russia, Iran, and North Korea — access to or control over critical infrastructure such as the power grid, water treatment systems, and communications networks. The prohibition applies to both private contracts and government agreements where such access is a term of the deal.
