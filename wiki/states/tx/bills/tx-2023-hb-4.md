## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

House Bill 4 of the 88th Texas Legislature, authored by Rep. Giovanni Capriglione with joint authors Oscar Longoria, Dustin Burrows, and Morgan Meyer and Senate sponsor Bryan Hughes, was filed on February 16, 2023, and referred to the House Committee on Business & Industry, which held public hearings on March 13 and March 20, 2023. On March 20 the committee voted 8–0 to report the bill favorably as a committee substitute, and the full House passed CSHB 4 on second reading (Record 83: 146–0–1) on April 4 and on third reading (Record 88: 146–0–1) on April 5, 2023, with a Statement of Legislative Intent entered by Capriglione and Rep. Ana Hernandez clarifying the bill's treatment of ERCOT electric utilities. The Senate Committee on State Affairs heard and voted out the bill 11–0 on May 4, 2023, and the Senate passed it unanimously (30–0) on May 10 after adopting a Hughes floor amendment and a Menéndez amendment adjusting the effective date; the House declined to concur with Senate amendments and requested a conference committee, which filed its report on May 26. The Senate adopted the conference committee report unanimously (31–0) on May 27, 2023, and the House followed (Record 2203: 143–0–2) on May 28, after which the enrolled bill was signed in both chambers on May 29, transmitted to the Governor on May 30, and signed by Governor Greg Abbott on June 18, 2023, enacting the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act with an effective date of July 1, 2024.
