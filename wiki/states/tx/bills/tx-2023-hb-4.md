## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

Texas House Bill 4, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA), was filed on February 16, 2023, by Representative Giovanni Capriglione (R-Southlake) with joint authors Oscar Longoria, Dustin Burrows, and Morgan Meyer — a low bill number signaling its designation as a House priority by Speaker Dade Phelan. Referred to the House Committee on Business and Industry on February 23, the committee held public hearings on March 13 and March 20, 2023, and reported the bill favorably as a committee substitute on March 20; the full House then passed it unanimously, 146-0, on second reading (RV#83, April 4, 2023) and 146-0 on third reading (RV#88, April 5, 2023). The bill was received by the Senate on May 1, 2023, and referred to the Senate Committee on State Affairs where Senator Bryan Hughes (R-Mineola) sponsored it; that committee held a public hearing on May 4, reported the bill on May 8, and the full Senate passed an amended version 31-0 on May 10. The House declined to concur in the Senate's amendments on May 15 and appointed a conference committee; the conference report was adopted by the Senate 31-0 on May 27 and by the House 144-0 on May 28 (RV#2204), and Governor Greg Abbott signed the bill on June 18, 2023. Codified as Texas Business and Commerce Code Chapter 541, the TDPSA took effect July 1, 2024; it grants Texas consumers rights to access, correct, delete, and port their personal data and to opt out of targeted advertising, data sales, and certain profiling; imposes controller duties including data-protection assessments and affirmative consent for sensitive-data processing; uses a small-business exemption keyed to the SBA definition rather than a numeric revenue or data-volume threshold; and is enforced exclusively by the Texas Attorney General — who must offer a 30-day cure period before suit — with civil penalties up to $7,500 per violation and no private right of action.
