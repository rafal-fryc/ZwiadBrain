## Summary

This law amends Texas Business & Commerce Code chapter 521 to tighten breach-notification obligations for persons doing business in Texas. After a breach of security of computerized data, covered persons must notify the Texas Attorney General as soon as practicable and no later than 30 days after discovery, submitted electronically through the AG's designated form.
