## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

SB 768, authored by Senator Tan Parker and sponsored in the House by Representatives Giovanni Capriglione and Suleman Lalani, was filed on February 7, 2023, in the Texas Senate during the 88th Regular Session. It amends the Texas Identity Theft Enforcement and Protection Act (Business & Commerce Code Chapter 521) to require that notifications to the Texas Attorney General of data breaches affecting at least 250 Texas residents be submitted electronically through a form on the Attorney General's public website, and to shorten the AG-notification deadline from 60 to 30 days after a breach is determined. The bill was reported favorably by the Senate Business & Commerce Committee on March 27, 2023 (10-0), and passed the full Senate on April 3, 2023. It was received by the House on April 12, 2023, reported favorably by the House Business & Industry Committee on May 5, 2023 (9-0), and passed the House on May 12, 2023 by record vote. Both chambers signed the enrolled bill on May 16, 2023, and Governor Greg Abbott signed SB 768 into law on May 27, 2023, with an effective date of September 1, 2023. The Legislative Budget Board's fiscal note found no significant fiscal impact to the State, as the Attorney General's office indicated it could implement the electronic-notification system within existing resources.
