## Summary

Texas SB 1343 (2025) amends the data broker chapter of the Business & Commerce Code (ch. 509). It expands the notice a data broker must post on its website or mobile application — a conspicuous notice stating that the entity is a data broker, together with information the Secretary of State prescribes for inclusion — and expands what a data broker's registration statement must contain, including contact details and a website address giving consumers specific instructions for exercising their rights. The duties bind registered data brokers.
