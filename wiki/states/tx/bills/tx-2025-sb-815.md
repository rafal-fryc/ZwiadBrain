## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

SB 815, authored by Sen. Charles Schwertner with coauthors Sens. Cesar Blanco, Molly Cook, and Bryan Hughes, was filed on January 16, 2025, in the 89th Texas Legislature, Regular Session. As enacted, the bill bars a utilization review agent from using an automated decision system to make, wholly or partly, an adverse determination on medical necessity or appropriateness of care — not merely as the sole basis for such a determination, but even as a partial factor — reserving that judgment for an individual (a licensed physician or other health-care provider), while still permitting automated tools for administrative support and fraud detection; it also grants the Texas Department of Insurance commissioner audit authority over insurers' use of such systems. The bill was read for the first time and referred to the Senate Business & Commerce Committee on February 7, 2025, received a public hearing on February 27, 2025, was considered and voted on in committee on March 11, 2025, and was reported favorably as a committee substitute on March 13, 2025. On March 26, 2025, the Senate suspended the regular order of business (25-5) and the three-day rule (31-0) to take up the bill, then passed it on a recorded vote of 31-0, including a floor amendment (FA1) offered by Sen. Schwertner. The House received the bill on March 27, 2025, and referred it to the House Insurance Committee on April 9, 2025; that committee held a public hearing on April 23, 2025, and reported the bill favorably without amendment on April 28, 2025. Rep. David Spiller sponsored the bill in the House, where it passed second reading 127-4 on May 23, 2025, and third reading 116-13 on May 24, 2025, with Rep. Richard Hayes as cosponsor. The bill was enrolled and signed in the Senate on May 26, 2025, signed in the House on May 27, 2025, sent to the Governor that same day, and signed by Governor Abbott on June 20, 2025, taking effect September 1, 2025, with its Insurance Code amendments applying to health benefit plans delivered or renewed on or after January 1, 2026.
