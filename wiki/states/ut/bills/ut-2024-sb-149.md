## Summary

Utah SB 149 (2024), the Utah Artificial Intelligence Policy Act, effective May 1, 2024, is the first US state AI-specific consumer-protection statute and works through the existing Utah Consumer Sales Practices Act and Utah Consumer Privacy Act. It creates a bifurcated disclosure duty: anyone using generative AI to interact with a person in a consumer transaction must, upon request, clearly disclose that the person is interacting with AI rather than a human, while a person in a state-licensed occupation (e.g. healthcare, legal, accounting, regulated by the Division of Professional Licensing) must prominently and proactively disclose use of generative AI in providing the regulated service. The law also clarifies that a person cannot avoid liability under Utah consumer-protection law by claiming that generative AI made the violative statement, and it establishes the Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy along with an Artificial Intelligence Learning Laboratory Program regulatory sandbox offering participating businesses reduced penalties or regulatory relief while testing AI systems, enforced by the Utah Division of Consumer Protection and Attorney General.
