## Summary

Utah SB 215 (2024), Motor Vehicle Consumer Data Protection, enacts a new Utah Code chapter (13-70) governing how motor vehicle manufacturers, franchisors, dealer data vendors, service providers, and other third parties may access, share, sell, or use "protected dealer data" stored in a franchisee/dealership's dealer data system. It bars franchisors and third parties from accessing, sharing, selling, copying, using, or transmitting protected dealer data without the franchisee's prior express written consent (revocable with 60 days' notice or immediately for cause), prohibits cyber ransom and unreasonable restrictions on a franchisee's ability to store, copy, or share its own data or integrate authorized third-party tools, and limits fees a franchisor or third party may charge for data access to actual costs plus a reasonable profit margin, disclosed in writing. It also requires dealer data vendors and authorized integrators to use standardized, STAR-standard-compliant data-exchange frameworks, allow franchisee audits, and ensure secure data transition to a successor vendor within 90 days of termination, and it makes franchisors/third parties indemnify franchisees for damages from violations of the chapter. The law took effect May 1, 2024.
