## Summary

Utah SB 98 (2024), Online Data Security and Privacy Amendments, effective May 1, 2024, amends Utah's Protection of Personal Information Act (Utah Code 13-44) and the Utah Technology Governance Act. It requires an entity obligated to notify Utah residents of a data breach affecting 500 or more Utah residents to also notify the Utah Attorney General and the Utah Cyber Center, with the notification required to include a description of the breach, the categories of personal information involved, relevant dates, mitigation steps taken, and contact information. The contents of these notifications to the AG and Cyber Center are shielded as protected/confidential records under Utah's GRAMA public-records law, and the bill builds on the Utah Cyber Center established by SB 127 (2023).
