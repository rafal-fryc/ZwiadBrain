## Summary

Utah's HB 444 amends the Utah Consumer Privacy Act, which as originally enacted applies to entities doing business in Utah with at least $25 million in revenue that process personal data of a threshold number of Utah residents; the bill title indicates further amendments to this consumer privacy framework, though the specific 2025 changes could not be confirmed from extracted bill text. It falls within Utah's existing scheme of consumer rights (access, deletion, opt-out of targeted advertising and sale) enforced exclusively by the state attorney general, with no private right of action.
