## Summary

Utah's HB 452 enacts a new Chapter 72a regulating AI-powered mental health chatbots, defined as generative-AI systems that engage users in conversations similar to a licensed therapist's confidential communications and that suppliers represent (or a reasonable person would believe) can provide mental health therapy. Suppliers are barred from selling or sharing a Utah user's individually identifiable health information or chatbot input with third parties (with narrow exceptions), from using user input to target or customize advertising, and must clearly disclose that the chatbot is AI and not human before use and periodically thereafter. The Division of Consumer Protection enforces the law with administrative fines up to $2,500 per violation and court-ordered penalties up to $5,000 per violation of an order, while suppliers who file a compliant written safety/privacy policy with the division gain an affirmative defense to certain unprofessional-conduct claims; the law took effect May 7, 2025.
