## Summary

Utah's SB 150 amends the Notice of Intent to Sell Nonpublic Personal Information Act, narrowing the definition of "commercial entity" to one that maintains a physical office in Utah and transacts business with in-state consumers there, and adding a definition of "physical office" that excludes P.O. boxes, mailing addresses, registered agents, or virtual offices used solely for correspondence. It expressly limits a consumer's remedy for a violation (failure to give the required notice before selling nonpublic personal information, with $500 in statutory damages per violation) to an individual action, declaring that the law's bar on class or representative actions has been a substantive remedy provision, not merely procedural, since January 1, 2004. The act took effect May 7, 2025.
