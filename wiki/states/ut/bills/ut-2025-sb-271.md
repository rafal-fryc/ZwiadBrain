## Summary

Utah's SB 271 expands the state's personal-identity abuse law to cover AI-generated impersonation, broadening "personal identity" to include an individual's video likeness, voice (including computer-generated sound attributable to a person), and audiovisual appearance, and defining abuse to include unauthorized commercial use of simulated or artificially recreated personal identities without consent. It also makes it unlawful to knowingly distribute, sell, or license technology whose primary purpose is unauthorized creation or modification of such content, while adding exemptions for news, entertainment, artistic, political, and newsworthy uses and shielding interactive computer services from liability for third-party content. Violations remain enforceable through the existing civil cause of action (injunctive relief, damages, exemplary damages, and attorney fees); the law took effect May 7, 2025.
