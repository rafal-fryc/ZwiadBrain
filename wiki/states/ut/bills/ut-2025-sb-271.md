## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

S.B. 271, "Unauthorized Artificial Intelligence Impersonation Amendments," was introduced in the Utah Senate on February 13, 2025 by Sen. Mike McKell, with Rep. Jordan Teuscher carrying the bill in the House. The bill expanded Utah's abuse-of-personal-identity statute to cover unauthorized AI-simulated use of a person's video likeness, voice, and audiovisual appearance, while carving out exemptions for news, entertainment, artistic, political, and newsworthy uses and shielding interactive computer services, and it created a civil cause of action for injunctive relief, damages, exemplary damages, and attorney fees. The bill moved through Senate committee review and Senate floor consideration in February 2025 before advancing to the House, where it underwent House committee review and floor consideration in March 2025, passing both chambers by recorded vote. Following passage, Governor Spencer Cox signed S.B. 271 into law on March 27, 2025 as part of a batch bill-signing, and the act took effect on May 7, 2025, consistent with the bill's own effective-date clause. The measure proceeded through the 66th Utah Legislature's 2025 General Session, reflecting concern over AI-generated deepfakes and unauthorized digital replicas of individuals' likeness and voice.
