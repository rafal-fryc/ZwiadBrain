## Summary

Utah's SB 332 is a narrow technical bill that further extends the repeal date of the state's Artificial Intelligence Policy Act, moving it to July 1, 2027 (superseding the date set earlier in the same session by SB 226). It makes no other substantive changes and took effect on a special date tied to the governor's approval or, absent a two-thirds vote, May 7, 2025.
