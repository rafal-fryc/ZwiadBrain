## Summary

Utah HB 320 restructures the state's Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy, replacing the prior regulatory learning laboratory application process with 'regulatory mitigation agreements' and 'joint interpretation agreements' between AI companies (participants), the Office, and the relevant state agency or governmental entity. Those agreements can grant temporary regulatory flexibility — such as cure periods or reduced fines — for deploying AI technology where participants meet eligibility criteria on technical capability, financial resources, and risk-mitigation planning, and accept required consumer disclosures and Office audits. Agreements run up to 12 months with up to two 12-month extensions. Took effect May 6, 2026.
