## Summary

Amends Virginia's breach-of-personal-information notification statute (§18.2-186.6) to add passport numbers and military identification numbers to the categories of personal information whose unauthorized disclosure triggers breach-notification duties. Businesses that experience a breach exposing these newly covered identifiers must now notify affected individuals under the same obligations that previously applied to data such as Social Security numbers and financial account credentials.
