## Summary

The Virginia Insurance Data Security Act adopts the NAIC model law for insurance licensees operating in the state, requiring them to develop, implement, and maintain a written information security program proportionate to their size and risk. Licensees must investigate cybersecurity events affecting their systems and notify the State Corporation Commission of qualifying incidents. The law places ongoing governance and incident-response obligations directly on entities holding insurance licenses.
