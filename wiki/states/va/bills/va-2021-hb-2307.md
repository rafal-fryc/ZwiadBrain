## Summary

The Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA), effective January 1, 2023, is the second comprehensive state consumer privacy law enacted in the United States. It applies to businesses that control or process personal data of at least 100,000 Virginia residents annually, or at least 25,000 residents if more than half of the business's revenue derives from selling personal data. Covered consumers gain rights to access, correct, delete, and port their data, and to opt out of targeted advertising, data sales, and profiling for consequential decisions; controllers must observe purpose limitation, data minimization, security safeguards, consent for sensitive data processing, and data protection assessments. Enforcement is exclusive to the Attorney General, with no private right of action.
