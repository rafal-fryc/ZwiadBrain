## Summary

Amends the VCDPA to address deletion requests where a controller obtained personal data from third parties rather than directly from the consumer. In such cases a controller may satisfy a deletion request by opting the consumer out of further processing instead of purging data the controller did not itself collect. The amendment provides a defined compliance pathway for controllers who cannot practically trace and delete indirectly acquired data.
