## Summary

Amends the VCDPA on definitions and enforcement mechanics, eliminating the previously established Consumer Privacy Fund (§59.1-585) and directing civil penalty proceeds into an existing state revolving trust fund instead. The amendment also expands the nonprofit exemption to cover political organizations and certain 501(c)(4) social-welfare organizations. The Attorney General's pre-suit cure process is not created by this bill — it already existed in the 2021 VCDPA (§59.1-584(B)) and is left in place.
