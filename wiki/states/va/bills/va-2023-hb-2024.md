## Summary

Bars the Commonwealth — its state agencies and political subdivisions — from publishing the personal information of active or retired Virginia judges and magistrates on the internet after the individual has submitted a written demand that it be removed. The prohibition runs only against public bodies, not against any person or private website, and does not extend to the officials' immediate family members. A violation is a criminal offense: a Class 6 felony, carrying a mandatory minimum term of six months' confinement where the publication is done with the intent to coerce, intimidate, or harass. The Act also adds judges and magistrates to the categories of officials eligible for address-confidentiality protection in the voter-registration records.
