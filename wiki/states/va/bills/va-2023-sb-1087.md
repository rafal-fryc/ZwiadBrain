## Summary

Establishes a genetic data privacy framework governing direct-to-consumer genetic testing companies doing business in Virginia. Companies must obtain consumer consent before collecting, using, or disclosing genetic data, implement reasonable security practices to protect that data, and honor consumer rights to access and delete their genetic information. Violations are subject to civil penalties, with enforcement directed at companies offering testing services directly to individuals outside a clinical or research context.
