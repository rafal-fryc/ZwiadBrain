## Summary

Amends the VCDPA to impose additional obligations on controllers regarding the personal data of known children — defined under the VCDPA as individuals under 13 years of age. Where a controller has actual knowledge that a user is a child, it must process that child's personal data in accordance with the federal Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), including obtaining verifiable parental consent before collection. The amendment extends the VCDPA's existing framework with these child-specific protections tied to the statute's under-13 "child" definition.
