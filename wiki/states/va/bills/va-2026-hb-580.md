## Summary

Virginia HB 580 expands the duties of the Division of Consumer Counsel to establish mechanisms for receiving and investigating complaints from Virginia consumers involving emerging technologies, including AI-related fraud and abuse, and to refer appropriate complaints to relevant federal, state, and local enforcement agencies. ⚠ Text extraction produced only a 'Chaptered' status marker, so this summary follows the official summary rather than the enacted text.
