## Summary

Virginia HB 581 expands the state's existing civil action for unauthorized use of a person's name, portrait, or picture for advertising or trade purposes to also cover unauthorized use of a person's voice or likeness, and adjusts the available remedies (including punitive damages) and the statute of limitations for such claims. ⚠ Text extraction produced only a 'Chaptered' status marker, so this summary follows the official summary rather than the enacted text.
