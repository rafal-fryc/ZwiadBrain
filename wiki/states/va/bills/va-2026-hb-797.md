## Summary

Virginia HB 797 directs the Joint Commission on Technology and Science to study the feasibility and impact of creating a framework under which a person or entity could act as an independent verification organization assessing whether AI models or applications adhere to specified standards. ⚠ Text extraction produced only a 'Chaptered' status marker, so this summary follows the official summary rather than the enacted text.
