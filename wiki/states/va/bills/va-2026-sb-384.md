## Summary

Virginia SB 384, the Senate companion to HB 797, directs the Joint Commission on Technology and Science to evaluate the feasibility and impact of developing a framework for any person or entity seeking to act as an independent verification organization that assesses whether AI models or applications adhere to specified standards. ⚠ Text extraction produced only a 'Chaptered' status marker, so this summary follows the official summary rather than the enacted text.
