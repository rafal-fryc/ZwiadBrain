## Summary

Virginia SB 753, the Senate companion to HB 581, expands the state's existing civil action for unauthorized use of a person's name, portrait, or picture for advertising or trade purposes to include unauthorized use of a person's voice or likeness, and adjusts the available remedies (including punitive damages) and the statute of limitations. ⚠ Text extraction produced only a 'Chaptered' status marker, so this summary follows the official summary rather than the enacted text.
