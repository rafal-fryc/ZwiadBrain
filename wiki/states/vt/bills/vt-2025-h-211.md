## Summary

Vermont's H.211, the Data Broker Act (Act 138), overhauls the state's existing data broker registry by broadening the definitions of "data broker" and "brokered personal information," tightening registration requirements, raising the annual registration fee from $100 to $900, and requiring brokers to post a $20,000 surety bond. It also adds a consumer-facing mechanism to request deletion of brokered data and expands public disclosure of the categories of personal information brokers collect, sell, or share. The law was motivated in part by concern over generative-AI systems becoming recipients of brokered consumer data. Signed June 16, 2026.
