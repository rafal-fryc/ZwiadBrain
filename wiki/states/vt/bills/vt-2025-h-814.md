## Summary

Vermont's H.814 establishes a statement of neurological rights in state law, including privacy protections for neural data, freedom of thought, and protection from unauthorized neurotechnological interventions, placing Vermont among a small group of states (with California's SB 1223 and Colorado's HB 1058) to enact neural-data privacy protections. The bill also extends the state's Artificial Intelligence Advisory Council through 2030 and directs study of AI use in health care, education, insurance, human services, and government, though those provisions are primarily government-facing rather than regulating private conduct.
