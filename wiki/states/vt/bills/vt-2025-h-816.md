## Summary

Vermont's H.816 (Act 156), signed June 17, 2026, adds 18 V.S.A. 7115, which bars a corporation or entity from providing, advertising, or offering mental health services to the public, including through artificial intelligence, unless the services are provided by a mental health professional or are part of an approved institutional review board or privacy board study. A violation by a corporation or entity is a violation of the Consumer Protection Act, with Attorney General and private remedies. Licensed mental health professionals may still use HIPAA-compliant AI tools and FDA-authorized digital therapeutics within their scope of practice if they review and approve the services, and engaging in prohibited AI use is made unprofessional conduct. The Artificial Intelligence Advisory Council must report on AI use by mental health professionals by January 15, 2027. The act took effect on passage.
