## Summary

Vermont's S.69 (Act 63), the Vermont Age-Appropriate Design Code Act (9 V.S.A. chapter 62, subchapter 6), signed June 12, 2025, applies to covered businesses that do business in Vermont, generate a majority of annual revenue from online services, collect consumers' personal data, and offer online products reasonably likely to be accessed by minors (under 18). Likelihood of access is based on indicators such as being directed to children under COPPA, or an audience of at least two percent minors aged 2 to 17. A covered business owes a minimum duty of care to covered minors, meaning its data use and design must not result in reasonably foreseeable emotional distress, compulsive use, or discrimination based on protected characteristics. It must set all default privacy settings for covered minors to the highest level, including keeping minors' accounts and posts on social media platforms hidden from known adults unless the minor expressly allows otherwise. Government entities, HIPAA-protected information, journalism entities, and GLBA financial institutions are excluded.
