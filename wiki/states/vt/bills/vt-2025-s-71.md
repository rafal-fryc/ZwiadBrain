## Summary

Vermont's S.71, the Data Privacy and Online Surveillance Act (Act 145), establishes a comprehensive consumer privacy law giving Vermont residents the right to access, correct, delete, and obtain copies of their personal data, and to opt out of targeted advertising, data sales, and certain automated profiling. It applies to businesses meeting the law's coverage thresholds and is enforced exclusively by the Vermont Attorney General, with no private right of action. Signed June 16, 2026, most provisions take effect January 1, 2028; it succeeds the 2024 comprehensive privacy bill H.121, which Governor Scott vetoed.
