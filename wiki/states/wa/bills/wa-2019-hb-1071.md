## Summary

Washington's 2019 breach-notification overhaul, effective March 2020, expands the definition of personal information triggering notification obligations to include full birth dates, health insurance and medical information, biometric data, usernames or email addresses combined with credentials, and government-issued ID numbers. Businesses must notify affected consumers and the Attorney General within 30 days of discovering a breach, reduced from the prior 45-day deadline. AG notification is required when more than 500 Washington residents are affected.
