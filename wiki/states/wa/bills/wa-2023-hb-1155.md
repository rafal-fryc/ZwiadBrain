## Summary

The My Health My Data Act, signed April 2023, establishes privacy protections for consumer health data that falls outside HIPAA's scope. Regulated entities must obtain consent before collecting or sharing consumer health data and a separate valid authorization before selling it; consumers hold rights to access and delete their data. The law prohibits geofencing around facilities that provide in-person health services. It is enforced by the Attorney General under the Consumer Protection Act and grants consumers a private right of action, making it the first U.S. privacy law of its kind for consumer health data.
