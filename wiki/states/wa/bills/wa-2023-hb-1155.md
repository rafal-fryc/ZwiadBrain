    9, 2023 by prime sponsor Rep. Vandana Slatter (D-Bellevue), at the request of
    Attorney General Bob Ferguson, and referred to the House Committee on Civil Rights

    comments in support, the committee approved a first substitute bill on February

    where the Law & Justice Committee held a public hearing on March 14 and voted

    response timeline was already in the House-passed bill; the Senate amendments did
    not extend it, clarifying only that authenticating a consumer's identity does not
    lengthen that period. What the Senate amendments actually added were the Act's
    phased compliance dates — March 31, 2024 for regulated entities and June 30, 2024

    amended measure passed the full Senate on April 5, 2023 by 27–21, and the House

    reproductive-health bills; it was enacted as Chapter 191, Laws of 2023, effective
    July 23, 2023, making Washington the first state to enact a standalone consumer
    health data privacy law.

## Legislative History

### Legislative Journey

Washington HB 1155, the My Health My Data Act, was introduced on January 9, 2023 by prime sponsor Rep. Vandana Slatter (D-Bellevue), at the request of Attorney General Bob Ferguson, and referred to the House Committee on Civil Rights & Judiciary. After a public hearing on January 24 that drew over 1,000 written comments in support, the committee approved a first substitute bill on February 3 by a 7–2 majority (with 2 without recommendation), and the engrossed substitute passed the full House on March 4, 2023 by 57–39. The bill crossed to the Senate, where the Law & Justice Committee held a public hearing on March 14 and voted to do pass as amended on March 22 by a 6–4 margin. The 45-day consumer-request response timeline was already in the House-passed bill; the Senate amendments did not extend it, clarifying only that authenticating a consumer's identity does not lengthen that period. What the Senate amendments actually added were the Act's phased compliance dates — March 31, 2024 for regulated entities and June 30, 2024 for small businesses — and a JLARC enforcement review by 2030. The amended measure passed the full Senate on April 5, 2023 by 27–21, and the House concurred in the Senate amendments on April 17, 2023 by 57–40. Governor Jay Inslee signed the bill on April 27, 2023 at a ceremonial signing alongside companion reproductive-health bills; it was enacted as Chapter 191, Laws of 2023, effective July 23, 2023, making Washington the first state to enact a standalone consumer health data privacy law.
