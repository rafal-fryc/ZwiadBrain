## Summary

Washington's Engrossed Substitute HB 2225 (chapter 168, Laws of 2026) regulates "AI companion chatbots" — AI systems with natural-language interfaces that give adaptive, human-like responses and sustain a relationship across multiple interactions — while exempting narrow business/customer-service bots, video game chat features restricted to game topics, standalone voice-assistant devices, and curriculum-aligned educational tools. Operators must clearly disclose that the chatbot is artificial (at the start of interaction and at least every three hours, or hourly and with added protections if the operator knows the user is a minor or the bot is aimed at minors), and are barred from letting the chatbot claim to be human; for minors, operators must also prevent sexually explicit or suggestive content and block a list of specified manipulative engagement tactics (e.g., prompting return visits, simulating distress at a user leaving, discouraging breaks, soliciting gifts/purchases). Every operator must maintain and publicly disclose a protocol for detecting and responding to self-harm or suicidal ideation, including crisis-resource referrals and annual reporting of referral counts, and violations are enforced as unfair or deceptive acts under the state Consumer Protection Act. The law takes effect January 1, 2027.
