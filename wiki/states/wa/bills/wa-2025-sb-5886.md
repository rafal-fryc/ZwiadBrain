## Summary

Washington SB 5886 (Chapter 69, Laws of 2026) amends the state's personality-rights statute, RCW 63.60, to add 'forged digital likeness' — an AI- or digitally-created visual or audio representation that is indistinguishable from a genuine depiction, misrepresents a person's appearance, speech, or conduct, and would deceive a reasonable person — to the existing property right individuals and personalities, living or deceased, hold in their name, voice, signature, photograph, and likeness. Unauthorized commercial use is an infringement subject to injunctive relief, a $3,000 civil penalty, actual damages or profits, and, for forged digital likeness specifically, non-economic damages regardless of profit. Took effect June 11, 2026.
