## Summary

Wyoming HB 102 (Enrolled Act 32) creates new felony offenses for knowingly distributing 'synthetic sexual material' — AI- or digitally-altered realistic depictions of a real, identifiable person's intimate parts made without consent and causing or intended to cause harm — punishable by up to 10 years' imprisonment and a $10,000 fine, or up to 12 years for repeat offenses. It separately makes it a felony to knowingly develop or distribute an AI system specifically designed to generate child sexual abuse material or to promote self-harm, carrying the same penalty. Schools, libraries, law enforcement, licensed healthcare providers, and interactive computer services hosting third-party content are exempt. Using AI to commit a crime is not a defense, and AI developers are generally shielded from liability for others' criminal misuse unless the system was built with the intent that its primary purpose be illegal. Took effect July 1, 2026.
